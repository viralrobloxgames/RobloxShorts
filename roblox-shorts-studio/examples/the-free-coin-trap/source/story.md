# The Free Coin Trap

Original vertical comedy with Max and Mia from the existing block-character library.
Saved George British narrator. Natural voice, playful music, coin chimes and impact sounds.
1080 x 1920, 30 fps. Final timing follows the recorded narration.

1. Immediate hook: Max beside an oversized mint coin dispenser. FREE COINS sign and tempting button.
2. A clear hand press releases one spinning gold coin. Max smiles.
3. Max keeps the button pressed. Coin rain accelerates and the pile grows around him.
4. EXPRESS DELIVERY lamp flashes. Coins pour from an overhead chute.
5. Buried up to his shoulders, Max panics; STOP / 1 COIN is out of reach.
6. Mia enters, pays one coin at the shutdown slot and stops the shower.
7. Max demands a refund. The dispenser drops one last coin onto his head. Mia laughs.

All animation baked as ordinary keyframes. No simulations or executable scripts needed on the farm.
Farm test frames first; full farm render after costs and budget are established.
Local processing is limited to scene construction, validation, audio, captions and encoding.
No local 3D rendering. No publishing.
