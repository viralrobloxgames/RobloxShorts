# The AFK Champion

Original vertical comedy with Leo, Max and Mia from the block-character library.
Same world as The Free Coin Trap. Saved George narrator (JBFqnCBsd6RMkjVDRZzb, eleven_multilingual_v2).
1080 x 1920, 30 fps. TikTok target: 63-66 s measured narration + 0.5 s tail (must exceed 60 s).
HUD top-left "ROUND n/5 · ALIVE n"; captions upper-middle; keep out of TikTok bottom 20% / right 15%.
