# Series: Every ___ Makes You ___

Format: each part is one Roblox game with one silly rule that changes your avatar (+1 size every jump, +1 speed every
step, ...), in the style of the "+1 every ___" games trending on Roblox. The rule is on a giant sign and a HUD counter
on frame 1. Leo and Max race to win under that rule, and whoever uses the rule hardest gets undone by it. A visible
payoff every 3-5 s, a twist planted earlier in the same video, then a teaser for the next game's rule and the call to
action. Each part stands alone: a new viewer only needs the sign.

Cast: Leo (orange hoodie, finds the exploit and pushes it too far), Max (teal, Leo's rival, schemes against him).
Mia and the noob can guest in later parts.

Running gags: someone gets undone by one last use of the rule; real obby rules (checkpoints, resets, kill bricks)
decide the twist.

## Devices to keep
- Rule sign on frame 1, Roblox leaderstats (Stage / Size) and a round timer all video, "+1 SIZE" pops, x10 fast-forward
  montages for big counts, a planted obby rule (checkpoints) as the twist.

- Every jump makes you higher: someone jumps out of the world into the skybox.
