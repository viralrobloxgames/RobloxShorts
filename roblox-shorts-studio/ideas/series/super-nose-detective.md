# The Super Nose Detective (series)

**Standalone episodes, not a part series (user, 2026-10-05).** Each video is its own case, "The ___ Case", watchable on its
own: no "Case 2/3" numbering, no cliffhangers that need the next video, CTA "Follow Viral Roblox Games for more cases".
Every episode has a **title card in the first few seconds**: "Detective" / "MAX SNIFFWELL" in the show's lock-up (white
condensed serif + big distressed yellow slab letters with a drop shadow, lower left, over a live scene) plus the case
name; the cover uses the same lock-up. Inspired by the original's episodes (see
projects/super-nose-case-2/source/research/dick-snifford-episodes.md).

Noir parody: Detective Max Sniffwell and the Super Nose gamepass ("I can smell anything on the map"). Narrated by
`detective_noir` (designed voice). Running gags: "Tempting. But I'm on a case."; three-part noir smell lists; donuts
are everyone's weakness; Chief Leo is the corrupt one; every case ends "Then my nose..." into the next case.
