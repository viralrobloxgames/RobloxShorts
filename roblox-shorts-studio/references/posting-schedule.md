# Posting schedule (2 a day)

Agreed plan from the analytics review on 2026-10-02 (Fri). All times are UK time (BST until 25 Oct, then GMT; keep the same wall-clock times after the change). The data behind it is at the bottom; re-check it after two weeks.

## The daily slots

| Slot | UK time | What | Why this slot |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | **12:30** | Story Short (Leo/Max/Mia/Noob) | TikTok follower activity peaks 12:00 to 15:00 UK (Philippines evening, US morning). Story Shorts live on For You (95 to 98% FYP), so they need the follower peak for the first push. |
| B | **17:30** | ViralRoblox News episode | Second-best historical TikTok window (16:00 to 18:00, 1.17x the account's normal). US lunchtime and UK after school. News is half Search traffic, so it's less time-sensitive, and the afternoon leaves the morning for research, narration (~20 min) and render. |

- **Weekends:** move slot A to **11:00**. Kids are up earlier, and three of the account's biggest 60-second videos were posted on weekend mornings (1.39M Sun 09:30, 565K Sat 10:48).
- **Same video, same time, both platforms.** Post to TikTok and YouTube within the same 15 minutes. YouTube Shorts tests a video over days, so the time matters less there, but there's no evidence it hurts.
- **Never post between 00:00 and 09:00, or 20:00 to 22:00.** Those are the account's weakest windows on TikTok (00:00 to 02:00 gets 0.62x normal views; 20:00 to 22:00 gets 0.75x). Part 3 at 00:19 and the Sep 13 post at 00:02 were in the dead zone.
- **Keep posts at least 4 hours apart** so the two videos don't compete for the same follower batch.
- **Series parts:** post one part per day in slot A, not three in 26 hours. Part 2 and 3 lost most viewers who hadn't seen Part 1 (full-watch dropped from 40% to 4% and 8%). Each part needs a stand-alone hook in the first 2 seconds, not "Part 2" alone.

## Scheduling

Render and approve the day's videos the evening before or in the morning, then use each platform's own scheduler so the slot is hit even if nobody is at the laptop:

- **TikTok:** TikTok Studio upload → "Schedule" (web lets you schedule up to 10 days ahead).
- **YouTube:** Studio upload → Visibility → "Schedule", set the same time.

Nothing is posted or scheduled without the user's OK on that video (see publishing.md).

## Review rule

Log each video's views at **24 hours** (TikTok and YouTube) in the same note, with slot, format and average watch time. After 14 days (about 28 videos), compare slot A against slot B for each format. If the other order wins by more than 1.5x on the median, swap them. Posting time is a small lever next to the hook; the data says retention decides reach.
