## 2026-10-05 (Mon, about 11:00 UK)

Covers the 13 new-format videos posted 30 Sep to 4 Oct, plus the whole TikTok history (695 posts).

### Findings

1. **YouTube is now the better platform for the story Shorts.** 13 Shorts got 18.8K views, 160 watch hours and 43 subscribers in 5 days on a channel that started at 2 subscribers. The same 13 videos got 17.4K views on a 25K-follower TikTok account, and 6.5K of those were one video.
2. **TikTok videos are failing the first test batch, and it's the opening, not the slot.** 12 of 13 sit at 350 to 1,600 views. Average watch is 12 to 24 s of about 66 s, and the finish rate is 1 to 8%. The only breakout, Admin Part 1 (6.5K), had 33.5 s average watch and 40% finish; the next best, the 1M coins noob (1.6K), had 24% finish. TikTok's own note on the weak ones: most viewers left at 0:02.
3. **The evening slots haven't delivered US viewers on TikTok yet.** US share on the 19:00 to 23:45 posts was 3 to 14% (target: over 20%). The Philippines share did fall to roughly 0 to 11%, so that half worked. Viewers are mostly UK, Germany, Poland and Romania. With under 1,000 views a video, the audience is mainly the first test batch (12 to 34% existing followers), so this isn't a fair test of the slots yet.
4. **YouTube reaches the US far better:** UK 22%, US 16%, Philippines 6% across all views. 92% of views come from the Shorts feed.
5. **Admin For One Round is the strongest concept on both platforms.** YouTube top three: Part 3 (3.6K, 89% viewed), Part 2 (3.4K, 79%), Every Jump Makes You Bigger (2.3K, 86%, and the best "stayed to watch" at 67%).
6. **Later parts lose people at the swipe.** YouTube "stayed to watch": Part 2 65%, Part 3 64%, Part 4 48%, Part 5 42%. Those who stay on Part 5 watch 83% of it, so the story holds; the "(Part 5)" opening doesn't. Standalone videos and Parts 1 to 3 are the better bets.
7. **Horror is the weakest story format on TikTok** (12.5 s and 13.9 s average watch, the lowest of the stories). On YouTube it's mid-table for views but brought the most subscribers of the stories (4 each).
8. **News #1 failed on TikTok** (584 views, 6 s average watch, 0.6% finish, only 40% of traffic from For You). On YouTube it got average views (956) but the most subscribers (6) and comments of any video.
9. **A trending game name helps search and US reach.** Stealing a T-Rex Egg (Steal an Egg) had the highest US share of the stories on TikTok (14%) and 25% of its traffic from outside For You.
10. **What built the TikTok account:** short clips on trending-game updates. 375 Grow a Garden posts (median 7.3K views, 23 over 100K), brainrot clips (median 79K), and 60 s Roblox news/drama, which earned £176 of the £177 Creator Rewards. The account has been cold since July 2026: monthly median views were 2.5K to 9K in the first half of 2026, and about 1K since, on 4 to 6 posts a month.
11. **Long-run TikTok timing is unchanged from 2 Oct.** Raw views are highest for posts at 12:00 to 15:00 UK (1.44x the account median) but that's the Philippines audience; 00:00 to 09:00 is worst (0.5 to 0.65x). Weekday makes little difference (0.86x Monday to 1.11x Saturday).
