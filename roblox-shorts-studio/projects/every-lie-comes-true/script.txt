Whatever Leo lies about comes true.
So when the teacher asks for his homework, he says, "A dragon ate it."

The window explodes.
A dragon eats his homework. Then the teacher's desk.

"I'm the fastest kid in school." Now he is.
"I've got a million dollars." It pours out of his backpack.
"Mia thinks I'm cool." She waves.

Then he crashes Max's brand new bike into a tree.

"Did you break my bike?"
Leo panics. "Dude, I've never seen you before."

Max stops.
He stares at Leo like a stranger.
Because now, he is one.
"Sorry... who are you?"

So Leo lies again. "You're my best friend."
It wasn't true anymore. So now it is.
Max grins and hugs him.

Then he sees the bike.
"Bro... who broke it?"

Leo almost says, "A dragon."
Something huge lands on the roof.
He shuts his mouth.

For the first time all day, he tells the truth.
"I did."

"Then you're buying me a new one."
"Fine. I've got a million dollars."

Two true things in one day.
A new record.
