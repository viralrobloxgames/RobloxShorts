# Every Lie Comes True (standalone story, one part)

Mode: power with a downside, third person (references/story.md). Script: `../script.txt` (187 words with the CTA;
expect about 72-75 s at George's pace, so over 60 s with room to spare).
Leo has the power; Max is his best friend; Mia waves; the teacher is the Noob. Dragon from the creatures pack.

## The rule (one, kept consistent)
Whatever Leo says that isn't true becomes true the moment he says it. True statements change nothing.
The downside: a lie doesn't add something, it overwrites what was real. Lie about not knowing someone, and you
don't. Fixing it takes another lie, which overwrites something else.

## Beats
1. Hook (frame 1): the classroom window bursting in, a dragon's head coming through. Leo, deadpan, "A dragon ate it."
2. Payoff of the rule: the dragon eats the homework, then the teacher's desk. Noob teacher frozen.
3. Montage (fast, ~2 s each): Leo sprinting past everyone on the track; cash pouring out of his backpack;
   Mia waving at him across the playground.
4. The crash: Leo rides Max's brand-new bike into a tree.
5. The careless lie: "Dude, I've never seen you before." Max stops dead, the friendly look drains out of his face.
   "Sorry... who are you?" Hold this; it's the turn.
6. Partial fix: "You're my best friend." It's a lie now, so it comes true. Max grins and hugs him.
7. Exposed problem: Max sees the wrecked bike. "Bro... who broke it?"
8. Temptation: Leo starts to say "A dragon." A huge shadow and a thud on the roof; dust falls. He shuts his mouth.
9. Earned choice: the truth. "I did." Nothing magical happens, which is the point.
10. Button: "Then you're buying me a new one." "Fine. I've got a million dollars." (It's true now, from beat 3.)
    "Two true things in one day. A new record." Leo hands over a wad of cash; Max takes it.
11. Spoken CTA + end card: "Follow Viral Roblox Games for more stories like this." @viralrobloxgames.

## Build notes (web route)
- New: classroom with a breakable window and the teacher's desk, a track or playground, a bike (whole and
  wrecked), a tree, a school roof. Dragon from `assets/roblox_pack/creatures` (check its scale against the window).
- A lie "coming true" gets one consistent effect each time (a quick sparkle or glitch on the thing that changes),
  so viewers learn the rule by eye; use the same effect on Max's face when he forgets Leo and again when he
  remembers.
- The sprint and bike ride are real locomotion (`travel`), not on the spot.
- No two-arms-up poses: Mia waves with one arm (`waveArm`); the hug is arm-around-shoulder, checked from the camera.
- Speech lines are in quotes for the narrator; consider speech bubbles for Leo's lies so they read on mute.
