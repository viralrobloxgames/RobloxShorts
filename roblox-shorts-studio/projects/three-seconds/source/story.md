# Three Seconds (standalone original story, one part)

A complete story in one video: no series, no Roblox-game premise. Max, Mia and Leo are just the actors.
1080 x 1920, 30 fps. Script: `../script.txt` (163 words, about 64-66 s at George's pace; Creator Rewards needs > 60 s).

## Brainstorm (2026-10-03)

What a standalone Short needs: a premise you get in one line (the hook), a rule that makes it hard, two quick
failures that teach the audience the rule, real stakes, a clever solution that uses something planted earlier, and a
twist that makes you rewatch.

Premises considered:

| # | Premise | Hook | Why not / why |
|---|---------|------|---------------|
| 1 | **Three Seconds**: Leo can stop time, but only for three seconds. | "Leo can stop time. But only for three seconds." | **Picked.** Understood instantly and on mute (frozen world, Leo moving through it). The limit drives comedy *and* the climax. The twist (Mia gets four) recasts the running fries gag. |
| 2 | The Ghost Who Couldn't Scare: a ghost has one night to scare someone or fade forever; the kid in the house just laughs. | "This ghost has until sunrise to scare someone. Or he disappears." | Strong and wholesome, but the ending needs a burglar and is more predictable. Keep for later. |
| 3 | The Last Stop: Max falls asleep on the last train; at every stop more passengers vanish. Twist: it is his surprise party. | "Every stop, someone disappears." | Good suspense, but the twist is a known shape and the train set is a big build. |
| 4 | The Coin That Always Lands Heads: Max wins every choice with a lucky coin until it lands on its edge. | "This coin has never landed tails." | Hard to show on mute; the ending is abstract. |
| 5 | The Lost and Found Box that returns anything you've ever lost. | "Whatever you lose, it gives back." | Heavier emotional story; harder to land in 60 s. |

## The rules (keep these consistent on screen)
- Leo's freeze lasts exactly **3 seconds**: an on-screen counter ticks 3-2-1 in the corner, the world desaturates to
  a cold blue-grey, everything stops mid-air (drops, pigeons, the ball, the piano).
- Three seconds is about **three steps**. Every freeze shows him getting about three steps, no more.
- Anything Leo lets go of stops the moment it leaves his hand; it carries on when time starts.
- Mia's freeze is the same effect in a **warm gold** tint with a **4** counter. It is only seen once, at the very end.
- The fries: Leo always has a red fries box. Whenever it is on screen after a cut, it has fewer fries. Nobody comments
  until the last line.

## Beats
1. **HOOK (frame 1):** a frozen schoolyard/park. A spilled drink hangs mid-air, a pigeon is stuck mid-flap, Max is
   mid-sneeze. Leo walks through it all, the counter at 3. "Leo can stop time. But only for three seconds."
2. **The catch:** "Sounds amazing. It isn't. Three seconds is about three steps." Step markers 1-2-3 under his feet.
3. **Fail 1, dodgeball:** a ball flying at his face; he freezes time and side-steps: one, two, three... colour back,
   the ball still clips him. BONK (cartoon spin, `dizzy`).
4. **Fail 2, pizza:** last slice on a table across the room; freeze; one, two, three, he has the slice in his hand...
   time starts with Max staring right at him. Caught (`nervous` face, Max `suspicious`).
5. **Plant:** quick shot of Leo's fries box, emptier than before. "He blamed Max." Max shrugs, innocent.
6. **Stakes:** a building with movers hoisting an upright piano to a second-floor window. The rope frays and snaps.
   Below it, Mia is scrolling on her phone. "Right above Mia. She didn't see it." Slow push-in, shadow growing on her.
7. **The freeze:** "Leo stopped time." Hard cut to blue-grey; the piano hangs a few studs above Mia's head. Wide shot
   with a dotted line and "20 STEPS" between Leo and Mia. He pushes at the piano: "way too heavy" (`push`, nothing).
8. **The idea:** camera drops to his feet: the dodgeball from beat 3. One: he picks it up. Two: he aims (`aim`). Three:
   he throws; the ball leaves his hand and stops in mid-air. The counter hits 0.
9. **Time starts:** colour snaps back, the ball zips across, BONK, Mia is knocked sideways out of frame, and the piano
   smashes on the spot she stood on (dust, piano keys flying, chord crash). Hold one beat of silence.
10. **Mia:** she sits up, looks at the piano, then at Leo (`suspicious`). "Did you just stop time?" Leo goes stiff,
    the frame goes to his face (`nervous`). "Leo froze. Nobody could know."
11. **The twist:** Mia smiles (`smug`): "Relax. Three seconds is cute." Gold freeze, a **4** counter. Leo is frozen
    mid-blink. "I get four." Mia strolls over, takes his fries, eats one.
12. **Payoff:** colour back. Leo looks down: the box is empty, Mia is back in her spot, chewing. "And his fries were
    gone again." Leo's face to camera, Max in the background looking vindicated.
13. **CTA:** "What power would you want? Comment below. Follow Viral Roblox Games for more." End card with
    @viralrobloxgames (no "part 2": this story is complete).
