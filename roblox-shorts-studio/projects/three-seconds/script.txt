Leo can stop time.
But only for three seconds.

Sounds amazing. It isn't.
Three seconds is about three steps.
He stopped time to dodge a dodgeball.
One. Two. Three. Bonk.
He stopped time to steal the last slice of pizza.
One. Two. Three. Caught.
And somehow, his fries kept disappearing. He blamed Max.

Then one day, a piano rope snapped.
Right above Mia.
She didn't see it.

Leo stopped time.
Mia was twenty steps away.
The piano was way too heavy.
But by his foot... the dodgeball.
One. He picked it up.
Two. He aimed.
Three. He threw it.
Time started.
Bonk. The ball knocked Mia clear...
and the piano smashed right where she'd been standing.

Mia looked at the piano. Then at Leo.
Did you just stop time?
Leo froze. Nobody could know.
Relax, she said. Three seconds is cute.
I get four.
And his fries were gone again.
