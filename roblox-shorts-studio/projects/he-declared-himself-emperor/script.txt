In eighteen fifty-nine, a broke man in San Francisco declares himself Emperor of the United States.
And the city goes along with it.

His name is Joshua Norton.
He was rich, until he tried to buy all the rice in town.
Then ship after ship of rice arrived, and he lost everything.

So he writes to the newspaper: Norton the First, Emperor of these United States.
The paper prints it.

He wears a blue uniform with gold epaulettes, and a feathered hat.
He prints his own money, and local shops accept it.
He orders Congress to shut down.
Congress ignores him.

One day, a police officer arrests him.
The city is furious.
The chief lets him go, and from then on, officers salute him in the street.

He orders a bridge built across the bay.
Decades later, it gets built.

When he dies, thousands line the streets to say goodbye.
