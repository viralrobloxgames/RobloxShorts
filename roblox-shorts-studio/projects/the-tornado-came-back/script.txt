A tornado wrecks fifty planes at an air base.
Five days later, two weathermen say it's coming back.

Tornadoes were called impossible to predict.
The weather service wasn't even allowed to use the word.

But the general is furious.
"If you can forecast rain, why not tornadoes?"

So they dig through old tornado records.
On day five, the weather map looks exactly like the night it hit.

The odds of another one hitting the same base?
Billions to one.

The general stares at the radar.
"Another tornado. Yes or no?"

They say yes.

Planes roll into hangars.
Everyone heads for shelter.
One weatherman goes home, wondering if he can get a job running an elevator.

At six o'clock, a tornado hits the base again.
Almost the same spot.

This time, they were ready.
The first official tornado forecast ever came true.
And tornado warnings were born.
