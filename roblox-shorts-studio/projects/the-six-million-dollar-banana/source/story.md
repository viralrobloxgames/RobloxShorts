## The facts (kept accurate; sources below)
- *Comedian*, by Italian artist Maurizio Cattelan: a fresh banana duct-taped to a wall, first shown at Art Basel Miami
  Beach in December 2019 (Perrotin). Two editions sold for $120,000 each at the fair (a third went to the Guggenheim).
- A few days later (7 December 2019) the artist David Datuna peeled it off the wall and ate it ("Hungry Artist"); the
  gallery replaced the banana. The buyer gets a certificate of authenticity and instructions for display; the banana
  and tape are replaced as needed (Wikipedia).
- April 2023, Leeum Museum, Seoul: student Noh Hyun-soo ate the banana; he said he had skipped breakfast.
- 20 November 2024, Sotheby's New York: sold to Justin Sun for $6.24m with fees ($5.2m hammer), against an estimate of
  $1-1.5m. The narration counts "one million, three million, five, six point two million" (the bidding passed those
  figures; the total with fees is $6.2m).
- 29 November 2024, Hong Kong: the buyer ate the banana on stage.
- The banana for the sale was bought from a fruit stand on the Upper East Side of Manhattan for 25 cents; the man who
  sold it, Shah Alam (74), told the New York Times he had no idea (reported by Artnet, ARTnews, AOL).
