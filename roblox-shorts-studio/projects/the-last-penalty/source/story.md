# The Last Penalty (standalone story, one part)

Mode: grounded action, second person (references/story.md). Script: `../script.txt` (177 words with the CTA).
"You" (the keeper) is played by Mia; the penalty taker is Leo; the referee is the Noob. Max is on Mia's team.

## The rule (one, kept consistent)
Leo can't help looking at the corner he's about to shoot at. That tell is the whole story: reading it wins the
first kick, he takes it away by closing his eyes, and without looking he can't aim at all.

## Beats
1. Hook (frame 1): Mia on the goal line, Leo at the spot, ball down, crowd behind. Stakes line: last kick of the final.
2. Setup: Leo never misses, but Mia has spotted his tell (cutaway: his eyes flicking to a corner, earlier kicks).
3. First attempt works: his eyes flick left, Mia dives early, glove slaps it wide. Her team starts running on.
4. Worsening: the whistle. She left her line early (keeper must keep part of a foot on the line until the kick;
   IFAB Law 14), so it's a retake. Team freezes mid-celebration.
5. Escalation: Leo knows he's been read. He closes his eyes. Her clue is gone.
6. Choice: Mia doesn't guess. She plants both feet on the line and waits (hold this; it's the decision).
7. Earned payoff: eyes shut, Leo scuffs the kick into the turf. The ball dribbles forward and stops against her boot.
   (In a shootout the kick is over once the ball stops, so this is a miss: she wins.)
8. Button: Leo opens his eyes still staring at the empty corner, waiting for the net to ripple. Hold the reaction.
   Closing line reframes the opener: "Every one he could see."
9. Spoken CTA + end card: "Follow Viral Roblox Games for more stories like this." @viralrobloxgames.
