# Forty Floors (standalone story, one part)

Mode: power with a downside, second person (references/story.md). Script: `../script.txt` (149 words).
"You" is played by Max; the window cleaner by Leo; the pigeon is a new prop.

## The rule (one, kept consistent)
You can swap places with anything alive you can see. You get their spot (and their stillness); they get yours,
including your fall. A swap never removes the danger, it hands it to whoever takes your place.

## Beats
1. Hook: your platform's rope snaps at floor 40. Falling. (Frame 1 is the fall.)
2. Rule stated.
3. First fix works for you: swap with the window cleaner two floors down; you're safe on his platform, he's falling.
4. He drops past you screaming. You swap back: he's on his platform again, you're falling again, lower.
5. Knowledge beat: every person in sight (a man at a window, a woman on a balcony) would fall in your place.
   Floor numbers count down on the facade.
6. Choice: a pigeon on a ledge around floor 15. Swap: you hang from the ledge; the pigeon takes your fall, drops for
   half a second, opens its wings. (Planted by "anything alive" in the rule.)
7. Earned reversal: the window cleaner you refused to drop lowers his platform and pulls you in.
8. Button: the pigeon lands on the rail beside you, unimpressed. Hold this reaction.
9. Spoken CTA + end card: "Follow Viral Roblox Games for more stories like this." @viralrobloxgames.

## Build notes (web route)
- New: tall building facade with floor numbers, two hanging platforms with ropes (one snapping), balcony, window,
  ledge, pigeon (idle, drop, wings open, glide, land), city street far below, sky.
- Falls are real motion (accelerating, then terminal), not slides. Swaps: an instant position exchange with a quick
  flash; the swapped person keeps your downward speed.
- No two-arms-up poses: falling uses `shock` (arms out at shoulder height); hanging is one/two hands on the ledge from
  below, checked from the camera angle.
