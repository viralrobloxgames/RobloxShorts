The window-cleaning platform snaps.
You're falling forty floors.

But you can swap places with anything alive you can see.
You get their spot. They get yours.

A window cleaner, two floors down.
You swap.
Now you're standing on his platform. And he's falling.

He goes past you, screaming.
You can't do it.
You swap back.

You're falling again. Only lower.
Thirty floors.
A man at a window. A woman on a balcony.
Every one of them would fall in your place.

Twenty floors.
Fifteen.
Then you see it, on a ledge.
A pigeon.

You swap.
Your hands slam onto the ledge.
The pigeon drops for half a second...
then opens its wings.

Above you, the window cleaner lowers his platform.
He grabs your wrist and pulls you in.
The pigeon lands on the rail beside you.
It doesn't look impressed.
