# The Guardian Quits (standalone)

Status: script draft, awaiting approval. No narration or rendering until approved.

**Logline:** Steal an Egg told by the guardian. A woolly mammoth guards one egg in the Snow biome and loses it every
day at the safe-zone wall (BONK). After 400 stolen eggs and a one-star review, he puts on sunglasses, a hoodie and a
"Player" name tag, gets waved through the safe zone, and steals every egg back from the players' bases. Leo chases him
and bounces off. The 400 eggs hatch at once into 400 baby mammoths, so he quits and takes a job as Leo's base guard
(snacks, treadmill, brainrot videos). His kids now guard the egg and bonk the wall just like he did.

**How it differs from the last two:** the narrator is the guardian, not a player. It's set in a daytime Snow biome
(new look). The heist is reversed: the guardian steals from the players. It uses new game mechanics (the safe-zone
wall from the guardian's side, player bases seen from outside, brainrot treadmill pets). The ending is a
circle-of-life callback (the baby bonk), not a reveal.

## Beats (~66-70 s)
1. **Hook (frame 1):** the mammoth stands over a glowing egg on a snowy hill. A player is already sprinting off with it in
   the background. "GUARDIAN" tag.
2. **The daily routine:** a quick montage of three chases (Max, Mia, a Noob). Each ends with the mammoth face-planting
   into the glowing safe-zone wall. BONK, BONK, BONK. A day counter ticks.
3. **The line:** the mammoth stares at the wall, confused. "?" over his head.
4. **400 stolen:** a stolen-egg counter spins up to 400. A boss card pops up: "GUARDIAN REVIEW ★☆☆☆☆".
5. **Disguise:** sunglasses, a hoodie stretched over a mammoth, and a "PLAYER" name tag. The wall scans him (green tick)
   and he steps through.
6. **The bases:** a wide shot of rows of player bases with his eggs on every pedestal. Leo's base has the most.
7. **The heist:** the mammoth scoops every egg into a huge pile on his back (carry drag "SPEED -99%"; it doesn't
   matter, he's a mammoth).
8. **Leo chases:** Leo runs at him and BONKS off the mammoth's side (role reversal). "Have you ever tried to stop a mammoth?"
9. **Home:** the mammoth sits on a mountain of eggs. The review pops up again: ★★★★★.
10. **Hatch:** the pile wobbles and 400 eggs hatch at once into a sea of baby mammoths ("x400"). The mammoth's face falls.
11. **New job:** Leo's base. The mammoth lies on a treadmill under a "GUARD" sign, snacks by his trunk, a phone screen of
    brainrot videos, "+$/s" ticking. Leo gives a thumbs up. "Best job I've ever had."
12. **Callback:** back at the snow hill, a player grabs the egg. A baby mammoth chases and BONKS the safe-zone wall.
13. **CTA:** "Follow Viral Roblox Games for more" + @viralrobloxgames end card.
