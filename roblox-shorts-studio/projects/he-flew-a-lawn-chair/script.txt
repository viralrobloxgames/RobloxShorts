A man sits in a lawn chair, tied to forty-two weather balloons.
The plan: float a hundred feet up, held by a rope.
The rope snaps.

A thousand feet. Five thousand. Sixteen thousand.
It's freezing.

He'd always wanted to fly, but his eyesight wasn't good enough.
He packed sandwiches, and a pellet gun to pop balloons.

Two airline pilots radio the tower: they've passed a man in a lawn chair.

He pops a few, and starts to sink.
Then he drops the gun.
He drifts into power lines, and a whole neighbourhood goes dark.

He lands unhurt, and the police are waiting.
Reporters ask him why.
"A man can't just sit around."

He's fined for flying an aircraft without calling the tower.
The aircraft is a lawn chair.

He was never... allowed to be a pilot.
Today, his chair sits in the Air and Space Museum.
