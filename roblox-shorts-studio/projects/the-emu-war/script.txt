Twenty thousand emus march onto farms in Australia, eating the wheat.
The farmers are old soldiers. They ask the army for help.
The army sends a major, two gunners, two machine guns, and ten thousand bullets.

Day one: the emus see them coming, split into tiny groups, and run.
Day three: a thousand emus walk right into an ambush.
The gun jams.

So the major bolts a machine gun onto a truck.
The emus outrun the truck.
And the ride is so bumpy, nobody can aim.

Every flock even has a lookout: one tall emu that keeps watch while the others eat.

After a week, two and a half thousand bullets are gone.
Most of the emus aren't.
In Parliament, politicians laugh and joke about medals.
The army pulls out.

And the major admits these birds could face machine guns like tanks.
The army went to war with the emus.
The emus won.
