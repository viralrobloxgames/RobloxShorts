At one in the morning, a guy who used to work at Google
types google.com into Google's own shop for website names.
It says: available. Twelve dollars.

He clicks buy. It goes through.

For one minute, he owns the most visited website on Earth.

His inbox fills up.
Messages meant for the people who run Google.
The site's owner controls, open on his laptop.

Then: order cancelled.
Twelve dollars, refunded.
Google wants its website back.

So he reports it to Google's security team.
They send him a reward:
six thousand and six dollars and thirteen cents.
Write it in numbers and squint.
Six, zero, zero, six, one, three.
G, O, O, G, L, E.

He doesn't keep it.
He asks them to give it to a charity
that runs free schools in India.

So Google doubles it.
Over twelve thousand dollars,
from a twelve dollar website.
