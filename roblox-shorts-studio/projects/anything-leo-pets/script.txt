Anything Leo pets with these gloves follows him. Forever.
A puppy. A kitten. A goat. A very angry duck.

They follow him to school.
Into class. Onto the bus. Into the bathroom.

He runs. They run faster.
He climbs a ladder. The goat climbs faster.
He gives the puppy to Max. It walks straight back.

So Leo stops petting things.
Hands in his pockets. Even at the zoo.

Then the lion gets out.
It charges straight at Max and Mia.
Leo jumps in front of them, and pets it.

The lion stops. Purrs. Rolls over.
Then it follows Leo home, like all the others.

Leo rips off the gloves and throws them away.
Mia catches them.
She slips one on, and pats Leo on the head.
"Good boy."

Leo follows her home. So does the lion.

Leo always wanted a pet.
Now he is one.
