# Anything Leo Pets (standalone story, one part)

Mode: power with a downside, third person (references/story.md). Script: `../script.txt` (154 words with the CTA;
The Backwards Umbrella was 152 words = 60.3 s of speech, so expect about 61 s of speech and a 62-64 s video: inside the
61-65 s rule). Cast: Leo (the gloves), Max, Mia, a puppy, a kitten, a goat, a duck, a lion. Route: web (three.js).
Pack animals available: `animal_golden_retriver`, `animal_cat`, `animal_goat`, `animal_lion`; the duck needs a stand-in
(`animal_chicken` recoloured, or a simple block duck in the clip kit).

## The rule (one, kept consistent)
Anything Leo pets with his bright yellow gloves follows him, forever: it walks after him at his pace, wherever he goes.
Petting is the only trigger, and nothing undoes it (taking the gloves off doesn't release anything already petted).
The downside: a growing parade he can't lose. The payoff uses the same rule twice: petting the lion is what saves Max
and Mia (and adds a lion to the parade), and the gloves work on whoever wears them, so when Mia pats Leo he becomes
the follower. The last line reframes the opening: he wanted a pet; now he is one.

## Beats
1. Hook (frame 1): Leo in bright yellow gloves pats a puppy; hearts pop and it trots after him. Quick cuts: kitten,
   goat, a duck that quacks furiously but still waddles after him in line. Reads on mute in a second.
2. School montage: the line of animals follows him into class (goat on a desk), onto the bus, and queues outside the
   bathroom door.
3. Attempts: Leo sprints (real run, distance-driven); the animals overtake him and wait at the corner. He climbs a
   ladder; the goat climbs it faster and is at the top first. He hands the puppy to Max; it hops down and walks
   straight back to Leo.
4. Hands in pockets, gloves hidden, walking stiffly past a petting-zoo sign at the zoo. The parade still behind him.
5. Danger: the lion's gate swings open; the lion charges at Max and Mia (shock poses, arms out at shoulder height).
6. Leo dives in front of them and pets the lion. It skids to a stop, purrs, rolls over; then gets up and joins the end
   of the parade. Wide shot of the walk home: Leo, puppy, kitten, goat, duck, lion.
7. Leo rips off the gloves and flings them; Mia catches them, slips one on, pats Leo on the head: "Good boy." (speech
   bubble). Hearts over Leo.
8. Mia walks off; Leo follows her, and the whole parade follows him, lion last. Hold the wide shot for the last line.
9. CTA: "Follow Viral Roblox Games for more stories like this." + end card (@viralrobloxgames, FOLLOW FOR MORE).
