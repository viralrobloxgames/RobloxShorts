The server said one player online.
So who was standing right in front of Max?
No name. No face. Just a light in its chest.
Max waved.
It waved back. Half a second late.
He stepped left. It stepped left. Late again.
It wasn't a player. It was copying him.
And every copy brought it one step closer.

The only door was behind it.
But a copy can only do what it has already seen.

So Max faked left.
It faked left.
He cut right and sprinted for the door.
It was still finishing the fake.
Then it ran. Half a second behind.
Max dove through and slammed the door.
It hit the other side. Hard.

Then... silence.

Max checked the player list.
Two players online.
Max.
And Max.

Then the lights went out.
When they came back, there were two of him.
And this time... neither one was late.
