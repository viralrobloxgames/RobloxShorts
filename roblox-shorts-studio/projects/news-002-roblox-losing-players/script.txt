Roblox has lost almost 30 million daily players.

This is ViralRoblox News.

Last fall, 152 million people played Roblox every day.
Now it's 123 million.
That's three drops in a row.

And Wall Street noticed.
Roblox's stock has crashed about 70 percent from its high.
On September 28th, the bank Jefferies said it could fall even further.

So why are players leaving?
Roblox now makes you pass an age check to chat, and Roblox says younger players were hit hardest.
Last year's giant viral hits have cooled off too.
But Roblox's CEO says a lot of it is just seasonal.

So what does this mean for you?
Roblox isn't dying. It still has more players than a year ago.
But expect big moves to win players back, like Roblox right in your browser.
