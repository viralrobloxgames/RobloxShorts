A man posts one red paperclip online, and offers to trade it for something bigger.
His plan: keep trading up until he owns a house.

Someone swaps him a pen shaped like a fish.
Same day, the pen becomes a doorknob with a face.
The doorknob becomes a camping stove.
The stove, a generator.

Trade by trade: a snowmobile, a trip, a truck, a record deal, a year of free rent.

Then he makes his worst trade yet.
An afternoon with a rock star... for a snow globe.
People online call it the dumbest decision ever made.

But one movie actor owns over six thousand snow globes.
He wants this one.
So he offers a part in his new movie.

And a tiny town in Canada wants that part.
They hold auditions for it, and pay with a house.

One year. Fourteen trades.
A paperclip became a two-story house.
