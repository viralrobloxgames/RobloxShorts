## The facts (kept accurate; sources below)
- 14 July 2005: Kyle MacDonald, a Canadian blogger, posted one red paperclip online (Craigslist and his blog,
  oneredpaperclip) aiming to trade up to a house.
- Same day: paperclip -> pen shaped like a fish -> hand-sculpted doorknob with a face. Then a Coleman camp stove,
  then a Honda generator.
- Later trades in order: "instant party" (left out of the narration: keg and beer sign), snowmobile, trip to Yahk
  (BC), a box/cube truck, a recording contract, a year's rent in Phoenix. The narration lists "a snowmobile, a trip,
  a truck, a record deal, a year of free rent" as a run of trades, not as direct swaps from the generator.
- He traded an afternoon with rock star Alice Cooper for a motorised KISS snow globe. Online followers were furious;
  one commenter: "This is probably the dumbest decision I have ever seen anyone make ... ever."
- Actor Corbin Bernsen collects snow globes (over 6,500, called the world's largest collection) and traded a
  speaking role in his film Donna on Demand for it.
- The town of Kipling, Saskatchewan traded a two-storey house (503 Main Street) for the movie role and held live
  auditions for it (won by local Nolan Hubbard). 12 July 2006: 14 trades, about one year.
- Narration names nobody (keeps the hook fast); names (Kyle MacDonald, Alice Cooper, KISS, Corbin Bernsen, Kipling)
  go in the post description. Staging only (not claimed as fact): who hands over each item, the faces, the house look.
