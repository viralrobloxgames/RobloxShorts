# Admin For One Round

Original vertical comedy with Leo, Max and Mia from the block-character library.
Saved George British narrator. 1080 x 1920, 30 fps. Final timing follows the measured narration.
Target just over 60 s (TikTok Creator Rewards).

1. HOOK (frame 1): Leo already floating above an obby course, a glowing ADMIN badge over his head, chat box open.
2. SPEED: Leo zips along the obby in a blur and lands on the finish pad.
3. GIANT: Leo grows huge; the lobby (spawn pad, sign, tiny Max and Mia) fits in his hand.
4. TINY: Max asks for a turn; Leo shrinks him to toy size. Max stomps, angry.
5. RAINBOW: Mia scolds Leo; Leo turns everything rainbow, including Mia.
6. EXPLODE (miss 1-3): Leo aims at the lava, the round timer, the leaderboard, the sky and a confused noob. Nothing happens each time.
7. SPAM: HUD timer shows 10 s. Leo types the command over and over.
8. REVEAL: Max, tiny, spots the corner message "EXPLODE - TARGET: ME x47".
9. PAYOFF: 3-2-1, ROUND OVER, ADMIN badge pops off. Leo gets launched by a chain of 47 cartoon blasts (bright flashes, no gore).
10. BUTTON: next round, ADMIN badge on Max. He types "kick Leo". Leo vanishes with a "Leo was kicked" chat line.

All animation baked as ordinary keyframes. No simulations or executable scripts needed on the farm.
Farm test frames first; full farm render only after a budget is agreed.
No local 3D rendering. No publishing.
