# Don't Sneeze Outside (standalone story, one part)

Mode: power with a downside, second person (references/story.md). Script: `../script.txt` (169 words with the CTA).
"You" is played by Max; Leo is the friend with the dandelion; Mia walks her cat (`creatures/animal_cat`, rigged).

## The rule (one, kept consistent)
Every time you sneeze, your gravity flips: down becomes up, and back again on the next sneeze. It only affects you
(Leo sneezes and nothing happens) and everything else keeps normal gravity (the pepper falls back on Leo).
Indoors a ceiling catches you. Outdoors nothing does, so the only way back down is another sneeze.

## Beats
1. Hook (frame 1): Max standing upside down on his bedroom ceiling; Mia's cat on the floor staring up at him.
   The cat is the plant for the ending.
2. Rule stated, and the danger sharpened: "Outdoors, there's no ceiling." (Cutaway: Max looking up at open sky.)
3. Coping: Max walks to school (real `travel`, not on the spot), cheeks puffed, holding his breath past flower beds.
4. Trigger: Leo, grinning, blows a dandelion in his face. Sneeze (SFX on "Ah... choo"). Max's feet leave the
   sidewalk and he falls UP, accelerating.
5. First fix works partly: he grabs a lamppost and hangs from it upside down, feet toward the sky, backpack and
   hood tugging skyward. He's stopped falling, but he's stuck: only a sneeze brings him back.
6. Second attempt fails by the same rule: Leo throws pepper up at him; it falls straight back into Leo's face.
   Leo sneezes; nothing happens to him. Max's grip slips (one hand comes off).
7. Help arrives: a tiny bell; Mia walking her cat. She lifts it as high as she can; it's well short.
8. Earned payoff: the cat climbs the lamppost by itself, right up to Max's nose (it was curious about him in shot 1).
   Sneeze: gravity flips back and Max drops onto the sidewalk, safe, flat on his back.
9. Button: the cat jumps down onto his chest. Max's face scrunches: "Ah... ah..." Cut on the inhale. Hold it.
10. Spoken CTA + end card: "Follow Viral Roblox Games for more stories like this." @viralrobloxgames.

## Build notes (web route)
- New: bedroom with a ceiling light (Max stands beside it), a street with sidewalk, flower beds, a lamppost tall
  enough to read as high up, a dandelion with a puff of seeds, a pepper shaker with a falling cloud, a bell collar
  and leash for the cat, open sky with clouds for the "falling up" shots.
- Gravity flips are real motion: accelerating fall upward, then a sharp stop at the lamppost grip. Upside-down
  shots: rotate the whole character, so hair and accessories stay fitted (run the fit check on the flipped pose).
- The cat climb uses the cat rig; it must visibly grip the pole, not slide up it.
- No two-arms-up poses: the lamppost grip is one hand (then the slip), the other holding the backpack strap; the
  sneezes use a head snap, not arms. Mia lifts the cat with both arms only to chest/shoulder height. Check every
  pose from the camera's angle, especially the upside-down ones.
- US spelling and words in captions and post text (sidewalk, color).
