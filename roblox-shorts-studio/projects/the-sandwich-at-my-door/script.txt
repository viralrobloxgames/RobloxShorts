Every morning for a month, someone has left a sandwich outside my door.
Always with a note: "Eat this. You look tired."
Peanut butter. No crusts. Cut into triangles.
Exactly how I like it.
Problem is, I live alone. Nobody knows how I like my sandwiches.
I asked my neighbor, Mia. She hadn't seen anyone.
I asked the mailman. He asked if he could have one.
Then on day twenty, the note said: "Stop asking people. Just eat."
So last night, I hid a camera in the hallway.
At three a.m., my door opened.
From the inside.
It was me.
Asleep. In my pajamas. Holding a butter knife.
I watched myself make the sandwich, cut off the crusts, write the note... and put it outside.
Then I knocked on my own door.
And went back to bed.
For a whole month, I'd been looking after myself. And I had no idea.
So tonight, I left a note for him on the counter.
"Thank you. You're doing great."
This morning, there was a new note on my sandwich.
"Thanks. We're out of bread."
So now I do the shopping. He does the cooking.
Best roommate I've ever had.
