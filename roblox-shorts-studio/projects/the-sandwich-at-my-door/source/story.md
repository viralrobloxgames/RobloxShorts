# The Sandwich at My Door (standalone)

Status: script draft, awaiting approval. No narration or rendering until approved.

**Logline:** For a month, someone leaves Leo a perfect sandwich every morning with a caring note, and he lives alone.
Nobody knows anything, and then a note tells him to stop asking. A hidden camera shows the truth at 3 a.m.: Leo himself,
sleepwalking, making the sandwich, leaving it outside and knocking on his own door. He writes a thank-you note to his
sleeping self, and the reply is "We're out of bread." They become the perfect roommates.

**Shape:** an everyday mystery with a creepy-turned-funny reveal and a warm punchline. It's not about a Roblox game; the
block characters just act it out (user rule, 2026-10-04). Narrated in the first person by Leo.

## Beats (~70 s)
1. **Hook (frame 1):** Leo opens his front door; a sandwich on a plate sits on the mat with a folded note. Punch-in on
   the note: "EAT THIS. YOU LOOK TIRED." On-screen day counter "DAY 30".
2. **The perfect sandwich:** close-up, triangles, no crusts. Leo looks around the empty hallway, suspicious.
3. **Lives alone:** wide of his small apartment: one chair, one mug, one toothbrush.
4. **Mia:** knocks next door; Mia shrugs (`shrug`).
5. **Mailman:** mailman stares at the sandwich and points at it hopefully. Leo pulls the plate away.
6. **Day 20 note:** a quick flashback card "DAY 20". The note reads "STOP ASKING PEOPLE. JUST EAT." Leo `shock`, music drops out.
7. **Camera:** Leo tapes a tiny camera to the hallway ceiling, then the shot switches to a night-vision camera view (green tint, REC dot, timestamp).
8. **3:00 a.m.:** timestamp 03:00, and the door handle turns from the inside. Hold.
9. **It's me:** Leo shuffles out, eyes closed (sleep face), arms forward, pajamas, butter knife. Hard zoom on day-Leo
   watching the footage, jaw dropped.
10. **Sleep-cooking:** a fast montage: spreading, cutting the crusts, writing the note, placing the plate, knocking on his own
    door, shuffling back to bed.
11. **Realisation:** day-Leo leans back, softens (`think` → small smile).
12. **Thank-you note:** at night, Leo props a note on the counter: "THANK YOU. YOU'RE DOING GREAT." Lights off.
13. **The reply:** morning, the sandwich and a new note: "THANKS. WE'RE OUT OF BREAD." Leo laughs (`laugh_big`).
14. **Payoff:** a split-screen montage. Day-Leo carries grocery bags in, and night-Leo (pajamas, eyes shut) cooks. A fridge
    full of sandwiches.
15. **Button:** "Best roommate I've ever had." Leo on the couch with a sandwich, raising it toward the bedroom door.
16. **CTA:** "Follow Viral Roblox Games for more" + @viralrobloxgames / FOLLOW FOR MORE end card (~2 s).

## Assets
Leo (day clothes + pajama tint; sleep/closed-eye face), Mia, a mailman (Max in a cap, or a generic noob). Built
procedurally: apartment hallway and front door, kitchen counter, plate + triangle sandwich, notes (text decals), a tiny
camera, night-vision overlay (green grade, REC, timestamp in overlays.json), grocery bags. Poses: `shrug`, `shock`,
`think`, `laugh_big`, `point`, `sit`; sleepwalk uses arms forward at shoulder height (no two-arms-up poses).
Standalone; no part tags.
