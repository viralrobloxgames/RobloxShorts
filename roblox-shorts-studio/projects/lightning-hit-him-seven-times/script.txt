One park ranger gets hit by lightning seven times.
And every time, it finds him somewhere new.

Strike one: lightning keeps hitting his lookout tower, so he runs outside.
Then it hits him, and takes a toenail.

Strike two comes through his truck window and burns off his eyebrows.

Strike three: his own front yard.

Strike four sets his hair on fire.
Now he carries a can of water.

Strike five: he spots a storm cloud and races away.
He steps out, sure he's safe.
Hair on fire again.

Soon, nobody will stand near him.
When lightning flashes far away, even his boss says, "I'll see you later."

Strike six gets his ankle.

Strike seven: he's fishing.
His hair catches fire, and a bear walks up to steal his fish.
He chases it off, still smoking.

Seven strikes.
He survived them all.
The record still stands.
