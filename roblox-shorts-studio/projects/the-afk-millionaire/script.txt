This noob won a million coins... without touching his keyboard.
Here's how.
Four players. One circle. Last one inside wins a million coins.
Leo wanted that money. Badly.
So he put a button outside the circle. Free coins.
Max lasted four seconds.
One down.
Mia was smarter. She didn't fall for buttons.
So Leo sent her a gift.
It was a bomb.
Mia sent it straight back.
Leo went flying. Two down.
Now it was just Mia... and the noob.
The noob hadn't moved. Not once.
Mia pushed him. Nothing.
She hit him with a sword. Nothing.
She fired a rocket launcher.
The noob flew across the map, bounced off a tree... and landed right back in the circle.
Still not moving.
Mia needed something bigger.
So she stepped out. Just for one second.
Three down.
The noob won a million coins.
Then, finally, he typed in chat.
"Sorry, was eating dinner. Did I miss anything?"
