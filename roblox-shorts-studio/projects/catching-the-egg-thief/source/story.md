# Catching the Egg Thief (standalone)

Status: script approved 2026-10-04; narration done; scene web/thief_clip.js; rendering.

**Logline:** Someone robs Leo's Steal an Egg base every night. The hooded thief has 50,000 speed and Leo has 900, so a
laser door and a capybara guard both fail. Leo stops trying to be faster and sets a trap: he spends everything on one Mythic egg
with a 60-second hatch timer and a "Do not steal" sign. The thief takes it right on time, and the egg hatches in their
arms into a giant spinosaurus. Pets know their owner, so it carries the thief back by the hood. The thief is Max, Leo's
best friend. Max repays the ten eggs on the treadmill in four seconds and breaks it.

**How it differs from Stealing a T-Rex Egg:** it's a night-time base-defense heist (player vs player, the game's
base-stealing mechanic) instead of a daytime guardian chase. The cast is a hooded mystery thief, and the guardian is a
spinosaurus (the pack's R15-style rig) instead of the T-rex. The new mechanics are speed stats, the hatch timer and
"pets belong to the buyer". The plan wins by brains, not teamwork; there's a whodunit reveal and a speed-callback punchline.

## Beats (~66-70 s)
1. **Hook (frame 1):** Leo at night in his base, ten empty glowing pedestals, red HUD "EGGS 0/10". "Again."
2. **Stakeout:** Leo's head pokes out of a bush. Moonlight, crickets.
3. **The thief:** a black hooded figure (nametag "???") blurs in, scoops all ten eggs in a stack and stops. A speed
   pill pops up: 50,000 vs Leo's 900. Leo chases, and one second later there's only a dust trail and Leo is panting.
4. **Night two:** a red laser door. The thief limbo-slides under it without slowing. Leo walks into his own lasers and gets
   zapped (tiny gag).
5. **Night three:** the capybara guard sits on a pedestal. The thief offers an apple and the capybara shuffles aside.
6. **The plan:** "stopped trying to be faster". Leo taps his head. The shop UI shows his cash going to $0 and one Mythic egg
   (the pack's hatchable egg) with a big floating countdown "0:60". The sign reads SUPER RARE - DO NOT STEAL.
7. **The bait:** the thief grabs it at 0:04 and sprints off across the lobby. Leo sits on a lawn chair and counts
   3-2-1 on his fingers. The timer is visible over the thief's head the whole time.
8. **The hatch:** the egg splits apart in the thief's arms and a giant spinosaurus unfolds, towering over them.
   "Pets know their owner": an "OWNER: LEO" tag pops above it.
9. **The walk of shame:** the spinosaurus lifts the thief by the hood in its jaws and strolls back. The ten stolen eggs
   rain out of the thief's backpack.
10. **The reveal:** the hood comes off and it's Max, with a sheepish grin. Quote card: "I was keeping them safe."
11. **Payback:** Max on Leo's treadmill. The cash counter rockets from $0 to "PAID", 4 s. Sparks, smoke, the treadmill
    launches Max into the air and he lands at the spinosaurus's feet. The spinosaurus growls and Max waves.
12. **CTA:** "Follow Viral Roblox Games for more" + @viralrobloxgames end card.
