# Which Max Rejoined? (After Hours horror, Part 2 of 2: the finale, v3 story)

Replaces the "mirror walk to the door" finale (`projects/which-max-is-real`, kept for reference). Picks up Part 1's last
frame (two Maxes; the real one is LEFT; the copy is a mirror image, hoodie star on the wrong side) and ends with a twist
that only a Roblox story can do: the game menu.

## Beats
1. **Hook:** two Maxes side by side, LEFT/RIGHT, PART 2 tag, player list "2: Max, Max".
2. **The test, done by Max himself:** he raises his right hand; the other raises its left. A mirror. "If you said left,
   you were right" (rewards Part 1's commenters).
3. **The threat:** no delay any more; it creeps closer each second (lights flicker, heartbeat).
4. **The idea:** it copies everything he does, so make it copy something it shouldn't. Max opens the game menu (an
   original in-world menu panel appears over him); a mirrored menu panel appears over the copy.
5. **Leave Game:** both cursors slide to LEAVE GAME. Countdown 3, 2, 1 (on-screen). Click.
6. **Disconnected:** both burst into pixels; black screen, "DISCONNECTED"; one button: REJOIN. Click.
7. **Joining server...:** a slow loading bar over black, heartbeat, silence.
8. **Spawn:** the empty lobby, a spawn force field, player list "1: Max". Relief: music resolves, Max exhales.
9. **The twist:** he looks down at his hoodie; close-up: the star is on the wrong side (this Max is mirrored). The
   copy is the one that rejoined. Hard sting, lights cut.
10. **CTA:** "Which Max clicked rejoin? Comment REAL or COPY" + follow card.

## Canon
- The copy got back in; the real Max didn't. The story ends there (no part 3 promised).
- UI is original story UI (not Roblox's own menu art); label as fiction in descriptions.
