# Feeding the Prism Kitsune (standalone, promo for Hunt For Eggs!)

An event explainer told as a story, for the user's own game **Hunt For Eggs!** (placeId 129859645860144). Every fact
below is read from the game's `ReplicatedStorage.GameConfig` (`PrismFeed`, `PrismFeed.Arrival`, `PrismFeed.Alpha`,
`PrismEgg`) on 2026-10-05; re-check them if the config changes before the video goes out.

- **Prism Feeding** runs 11:00 BST Sat 3 Oct to **10:20 BST Fri 9 Oct 2026**. The video has to be posted before then
  ("leaves this Friday" is only true up to Thu 8 Oct; from Friday morning it is dead).
- The Prism Kitsune drops from the sky (220 studs) into the plaza and sits on a plinth, 26 studs across.
- Each dawn some beasts are Prism-touched: crystal-studded, under a glowing beam. Their egg is a Prism egg.
- Feed it **5** Prism eggs = a Prism Chest: 2X Training 40%, 2X Coins 30%, Prism Egg 25%, **Prism Blade 5%**.
  Boosts last 15 minutes.
- Every **30 min** the Kitsune hops off its plinth and walks the whole corridor; every beast in a zone it enters turns Prism.
- Every **hour** a giant **Prism Alpha** lands (60 s warning). The whole server fights it; felled, it bursts into loose
  Prism eggs (at least 2, 0.75 per player) that anyone can grab.
