# Detective Max Sniffwell: The Vampire Case

Standalone case (no numbering, no cliffhanger; see ideas/series/super-nose-detective.md). A clean take on the original's
**Vampire Case** (2nd biggest episode, 820K Instagram views), built on the **Jacuzzi Case** rules from
`research/dick-snifford-episodes-watched.md`: flirt and catchphrase in the first seconds, title card at ~3 s, the
catchphrase three times, one long decisive sniff, a three-item smell list that holds the clue, and a corrupt Chief at
leisure planted in plain sight before he's the answer. Same voice (`detective_noir`), same outfit everywhere.

**Target 62 s** (user, 2026-10-05): `script.txt` is 147 words, ~62 s at Case 1's pace (172 words, 73.7 s). Something
new on screen every 2-3 s.

History: v1 "The Big Cheese" rejected (not standalone). v2 (221 words, ~94 s) too long, and its hook ("Tempting. But I'm
on a case." / "I wasn't on a case.") didn't make sense. v3 (62 s) still didn't make sense to the user: random elements
(the "camping" stake joke, the respawn, the floating ending). **v4 is one clue chain the viewer can follow:** garlic stops
vampires -> the garlic bread smells of a barbecue and sunscreen -> vampires don't wear sunscreen -> the only person
grilling today is the Chief, who already tried to bribe Max with a burger -> his motive is on screen (the mansion shades
his pool). Every clue is shown before Max says it out loud.

## Beats

1. **Hook (0-3 s).** Frame 1: the haunted mansion's door swings open on the sunny beach; Vlad's pale sister, fangs,
   bubble "Are you single?" Max in profile, nose first. *"The vampire's sister asked if I was single. Tempting. But I'm
   on a case."*
2. **Title card (~3-6 s).** Lock-up lower left ("Detective" / "MAX SNIFFWELL" / "The Vampire Case") over Max walking
   past her into the candlelit hall.
3. **The victim (6-14 s).** Count Vlad flat in his open coffin, cape, KO stars. Two 1 s flashback cuts under "nicest":
   Vlad holding an umbrella over a kid, handing back a puppy. Push in: a slice of garlic bread on the coffin's edge, green
   stink lines, Vlad's face turning greener. *"Her brother, Count Vlad. Nicest vampire in town. Knocked out cold. Next to
   his coffin, a slice of garlic bread. Somebody knew how to stop a vampire."*
4. **The Chief, planted (14-19 s).** Leo on a pool floatie in his pool right behind the mansion, white sunscreen on his
   nose, barbecue smoking at the poolside; he holds out a burger on a spatula. The pool sits in the mansion's shadow
   (he's in the shade; everyone else on the beach is in the sun). *"The Chief offered me a burger to drop it. Tempting.
   But I'm on a case."*
5. **The sniff (19-30 s).** Max picks up the garlic bread (evidence bag) and sniffs it. Extreme close-up, nose in
   profile, held a beat too long while the officer leans away. *"So I sniffed the bread. Longer than anyone should."*
   Three quick inserts, one per item: butter dripping, grill marks, a white smear of sunscreen on the crust. *"Extra
   butter. Fresh off a barbecue. And a hint of sunscreen."* Cut to Vlad's family hiding from a sunbeam. *"Vampires don't
   wear sunscreen."*
6. **The reveal (30-38 s).** Max turns slowly toward the window: the only smoke on the beach rises from Leo's barbecue.
   Smash cut to Leo frozen mid-bite, sunscreen on his nose, a tray of garlic bread next to the grill. *"Only one guy in
   town was grilling today. Chief Leo. Sunscreen on his nose. A tray of garlic bread."*
7. **The motive (38-44 s).** Wide shot: the mansion's huge shadow covers Leo's pool; Leo shivers on his floatie, shakes
   a fist at the mansion. *"Vlad's mansion shaded his pool. So Vlad had to go."*
8. **The arrest (44-48 s).** Max wades in; the cuffs snap on while Leo is still sitting on the floatie; Max tows him out
   by it. *"Cuffs on. Floatie and all."*
9. **Vlad wakes (48-52 s).** The evidence bag seals the garlic bread; the green stink lines fade; Vlad's eyes snap open
   and he sits up in the coffin. *"With the garlic gone, Vlad woke up."*
10. **Ending (52-60 s).** The family gathers round Max, warm and grateful, table set, bubble "Stay for dinner?" Max
    smiles... then they all smile back with fangs. *"His family asked me to stay for dinner. Then I saw the fangs.
    Tempting. But I'm on a case."* Max backs out the door and sprints down the beach.
11. **CTA (60-62 s).** *"Follow Viral Roblox Games for more cases."* End card @viralrobloxgames.

Content line: no swearing, no alcohol, flirting stays at "Are you single?". Nobody gets hurt beyond KO stars.
