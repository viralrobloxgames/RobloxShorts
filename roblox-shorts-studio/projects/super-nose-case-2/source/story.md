# Detective Max Sniffwell: The Vampire Case

Standalone case (no numbering, no cliffhanger; see ideas/series/super-nose-detective.md). A clean take on the original's
**Vampire Case** (2nd biggest episode, 820K Instagram views), built on the **Jacuzzi Case** rules from
`research/dick-snifford-episodes-watched.md`: flirt and catchphrase in the first seconds, title card at ~3 s, the
catchphrase three times, one long decisive sniff, a three-item smell list that holds the clue, and a corrupt Chief at
leisure as the red herring, and the real culprit planted in the background of the first shot. Same voice (`detective_noir`), same outfit everywhere.

**Target 62 s** (user, 2026-10-05): `script.txt` is 148 words, ~62 s at Case 1's pace (172 words, 73.7 s). Something
new on screen every 2-3 s.

History: v1 "The Big Cheese" rejected (not standalone). v2 (221 words, ~94 s) too long, and its hook ("Tempting. But I'm
on a case." / "I wasn't on a case.") didn't make sense. v3 (62 s) still didn't make sense to the user: random elements
(the "camping" stake joke, the respawn, the floating ending). **v4 is one clue chain the viewer can follow:** garlic stops
vampires -> the garlic bread smells of a barbecue and sunscreen -> vampires don't wear sunscreen -> the only person
grilling today is the Chief, who already tried to bribe Max with a burger -> his motive is on screen (the mansion shades
his pool). Every clue is shown before Max says it out loud.

**v5 (user, 2026-10-05): not the same culprit every time.** The culprit is now **the gardener**, planted in the background
of the first shot (the original's rule: the culprit is the least threatening figure, shown as a background joke before
anyone suspects him). The Chief's burger bribe stays, so he becomes the red herring: he's just lazy. Clue chain: garlic
stops vampires -> the bread smells of butter, fresh garlic and compost -> the garlic was homegrown -> only the gardener
grows anything -> a garlic patch behind the roses -> motive on screen: Vlad keeps the whole garden in the shade, and the
gardener's tomatoes have been sad for twenty years.

## Beats

1. **Hook (0-3 s).** Frame 1: the haunted mansion's door swings open; Vlad's pale sister, fangs, bubble "Are you
   single?" Max in profile, nose first. **Plant:** behind Max, in the gloomy shaded garden, the gardener (straw hat,
   green gloves, hedge clippers) snips a hedge and glares at the house; a row of droopy grey tomato plants beside him.
   *"The vampire's sister asked if I was single. Tempting. But I'm on a case."*
2. **Title card (~3-6 s).** Lock-up lower left ("Detective" / "MAX SNIFFWELL" / "The Vampire Case") over Max walking
   past her into the candlelit hall.
3. **The victim (6-14 s).** Count Vlad flat in his open coffin, cape, KO stars. Two 1 s flashback cuts under "nicest":
   Vlad holding an umbrella over a kid, handing back a puppy. Push in: a slice of garlic bread on the coffin's edge, green
   stink lines, Vlad's face turning greener. *"Her brother, Count Vlad. Nicest vampire in town. Knocked out cold. Next to
   his coffin, a slice of garlic bread. Somebody knew how to stop a vampire."*
4. **The Chief, red herring (14-19 s).** Chief Leo strolls in with a pool ring round his waist and sunglasses, holds out
   a burger: bubble "Nobody cares. Drop it." Max holds up one hand. *"The Chief offered me a burger to drop it. Tempting.
   But I'm on a case."*
5. **The sniff (19-29 s).** Max picks up the garlic bread and sniffs it. Extreme close-up, nose in profile, held a beat
   too long while the officer leans away. *"So I sniffed the bread. Longer than anyone should."* Three quick inserts, one
   per item: butter dripping, a chunky fresh garlic clove, a crumb of soil. *"Extra butter. Fresh garlic. And a hint of
   compost."* Max narrows his eyes. *"This garlic was homegrown."*
6. **The reveal (29-37 s).** Max turns slowly to the window: the gardener outside, the same shot as the hook. Smash cut:
   behind the rose bushes, a secret garlic patch, one bulb freshly dug up; the gardener freezes mid-snip. *"Only one guy
   here grows anything. The gardener. A garlic patch behind the roses."*
7. **The motive (37-44 s).** Wide shot: the mansion's huge shadow over the whole garden, the sun shining everywhere else
   on the beach. The gardener holds up one tiny grey tomato and his lip wobbles. *"Vlad kept the whole garden in the
   shade. Twenty years of sad tomatoes. So Vlad had to go."*
8. **The arrest (44-48 s).** The cuffs snap on over his big green gloves; the clippers drop into the roses. *"Cuffs on.
   Gloves and all."*
9. **Vlad wakes (48-52 s).** The evidence bag seals the garlic bread; the green stink lines fade; Vlad's eyes snap open
   and he sits up in the coffin. *"With the garlic gone, Vlad woke up."*
10. **Ending (52-60 s).** The family gathers round Max, warm and grateful, table set, bubble "Stay for dinner?" Max
    smiles... then they all smile back with fangs. *"His family asked me to stay for dinner. Then I saw the fangs.
    Tempting. But I'm on a case."* Max backs out the door and sprints down the beach.
11. **CTA (60-62 s).** *"Follow Viral Roblox Games for more cases."* End card @viralrobloxgames.

Content line: no swearing, no alcohol, flirting stays at "Are you single?". Nobody gets hurt beyond KO stars.
