She survived the Titanic.
Then she got on another ship.

Violet Jessop works on ocean liners.
In 1911 her ship, the Olympic, collides with a navy warship. Nobody dies. She keeps working.

The next year she joins the Olympic's brand new sister ship: the Titanic.
It hits an iceberg.
As she climbs into lifeboat sixteen, an officer hands her a baby.
She holds it for hours, until a rescue ship arrives.

Most people would never go to sea again.
Violet becomes a nurse on a hospital ship, the Britannic: the Titanic's other sister.

In 1916 it hits a mine and sinks in under an hour.
Her lifeboat gets pulled toward the giant propellers.
She jumps into the sea, hits her head... and survives. Again.

She keeps working at sea for another thirty years.
People call her Miss Unsinkable.
