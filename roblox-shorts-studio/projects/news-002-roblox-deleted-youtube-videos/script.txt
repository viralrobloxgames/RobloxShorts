Roblox just deleted every YouTube video from its game pages.

This is ViralRoblox News.

On September 30th, every YouTube video on a game page was wiped.
Creators haven't been able to add new ones since last November.

Now game videos get uploaded straight to Roblox instead, for free.
Roblox says that way, the video actually shows the game you're about to join.

But here's the twist.
Roblox says those videos are coming to Moments, its own TikTok-style video feed.
And this month, you'll be able to tap any avatar in Moments and buy what they're wearing, even the background.

So what does this mean for you?
See a fit you love in a clip? One tap, and it's yours.
Looks like Roblox wants you watching on Roblox, not YouTube.
