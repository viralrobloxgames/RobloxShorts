# He Ordered A Fighter Jet From Pepsi: resume notes

Standalone true story (one part), the next of OddFrame's best-performing Shorts after Google and the paperclip
("Pepsi Points fighter jet", 26K views). In 1996 a 21-year-old business student saw a Pepsi Stuff ad offer a Harrier
jet for 7,000,000 Pepsi Points, read the rule that extra points cost 10 cents, got five investors to front $700,000
and mailed 15 points and a $700,008.50 check. Pepsi sent it back with coupons, a judge ruled in 1999 that no
reasonable person would take the jet as a real offer, and Pepsi re-ran the ad at 700,000,000 points with "just
kidding". Leo plays the student; Max is the kid in the ad; Mia is the judge. Facts, beats and sources:
`source/story.md`.
