# Detective Max Sniffwell: The Clown Case

Standalone case (series rules: ideas/series/super-nose-detective.md). Inspired by the original's **The Election Case**
(projects/super-nose-case-2/source/research/dick-snifford-episodes-watched.md, section 1: the candidate is literally a
clown and nobody remarks on it; the "kidnapped" wife faked it; one sniff names the place; food stuck on the nose through
a tense scene; the detective returns her anyway and gets beaten up for it). Ours swaps their taco joint for an ice cream
parlour and keeps the Jacuzzi Case skeleton the research calls the template: flirt + catchphrase by 5 s, title at ~6 s,
case by ~20 s, one decisive sniff by ~25 s with a three-item tasting note, the catchphrase three times with the third
broken, a coda that undercuts the narration.

Voice: `detective_noir`. 158 words, ~63 s. Cast: Max; Skye (the girl at the beach bar); Chief Leo (briefs Max while
putting golf balls on the dock: the boss is never at a desk); Mr. Giggles the candidate (a second Leo rig: white face,
red nose, orange clown wig, a serious navy suit and striped tie, played completely straight); Mrs. Giggles (Mia rig:
big hair, bright dress, gold earrings); Big Scoop, the parlour owner, and his crew (the Noob in an apron and paper hat,
plus two crew in matching aprons).

## Beats (hook first; every beat reads on mute)

1. **Hook (0-3 s).** Beach bar at sunset, both faces 3/4 to camera: Skye leans on the bamboo counter beside Max, heart
   eyes, bubble "Are you single, detective?". *"A girl at the beach bar asked if I was single."*
2. **Catchphrase + title (3-8 s).** Extreme close-up of Max's profile, the nose filling a third of the frame, one hand
   up. TEMPTATIONS RESISTED: 1. The **Detective / MAX SNIFFWELL / The Clown Case** lock-up lower left at ~5.5 s over the
   cut to the case. *"Tempting. But I'm on a case."*
3. **The case (8-16 s).** The marina: Chief Leo putting on a strip of fake grass along the dock, golf glove on, holding up
   a photo of Mrs. Giggles; a "VOTE GIGGLES" banner on a boat. *"Somebody kidnapped the candidate's wife. On election
   day. The Chief wanted her back by the speech."*
4. **The clown reveal (16-20 s).** Campaign trailer, bulb-lit mirror: Mr. Giggles in full clown make-up and a sober
   suit, deadly serious, practising a smile; balloons. Max and the Chief stand there as if nothing is odd. *"The
   candidate was a clown. Not a figure of speech."*
5. **The sniff (20-29 s).** The ransom note in Max's hand ("$10,000 OR ELSE"); extreme close-up, held a beat too long,
   the clown watching, appalled. Three little icons pop as he names them (a waffle cone, hot fudge, a mint chip scoop).
   *"The ransom note wanted ten thousand dollars. I sniffed it. Waffle cone. Hot fudge. And a hint of mint choc chip."*
6. **The drive (29-33 s).** The pink convertible past palms to a pastel parlour with a giant cone on the roof: BIG SCOOP'S.
   *"Only one place in town smells like that."*
7. **The parlour + temptation 2 (33-41 s).** Inside: four guys in matching aprons counting cash by a shiny truck brochure.
   Big Scoop slides a towering sundae across the counter ("On the house"). Max leans in to sniff it and comes up with a
   **blob of whipped cream on the tip of his nose**, which he doesn't notice (it stays through the next beat). TEMPTATIONS
   RESISTED: 2. *"Big Scoop's Ice Cream. Full of suspicious guys. The owner offered me a free sundae. Tempting. But I'm on
   a case."*
8. **The twist (41-49 s).** Out back: Mrs. Giggles happily working the till in an apron, an "ICE CREAM TRUCK: $10,000"
   poster behind her, a picture of the truck taped to the register. She points at the cream on his nose; he wipes it.
   *"Out back, I found the wife. Not tied up. Running the till. She'd faked the kidnapping. The ransom was for an ice
   cream truck."*
9. **The scuffle (49-53 s).** Real action with space between them: the crew wind up, a sundae flies across the room and
   splats on Max's face (camera shake, sprinkles), then he walks her out by the elbow. *"Her crew objected. One flying
   sundae later, I took her back anyway."*
10. **The rally (53-58 s).** Stage with "VOTE GIGGLES" banners, balloons, a crowd in red clown noses. Mrs. Giggles at the
    microphone with a huge, fixed, painted-looking smile; a WINNER banner drops. *"She gave the speech with a very big
    smile. The clown won."*
11. **Coda + broken catchphrase (58-62 s).** Max alone at the beach bar, one black eye, the sun going down. Big Scoop's
    van pulls up; a sundae slides down the counter to him. He looks at it. TEMPTATIONS RESISTED counter glitches; he picks
    up the spoon. *"I got a black eye. And a free sundae. Tempting."*
12. **CTA.** *"Follow Viral Roblox Games for more cases."* End card: MORE CASES... @viralrobloxgames.

Content line: flirting only (Skye); no swearing, no sexual references; the "kidnapping" is fake and played for laughs;
the scuffle is slapstick (a sundae in the face). Held props to hold-check: the photo, the golf putter, the ransom note, the
sundae (Big Scoop's hands, then Max's spoon), the microphone.
