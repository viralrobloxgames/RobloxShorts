Max got admin for one round.
He only wanted one thing. Revenge.
Command one: kick Leo.
Leo was gone. For four whole seconds.
Then he rejoined.
Command two: jail.
A cage dropped right on top of Leo.
He walked out through the gap.
Command three: freeze.
Leo turned into a block of ice.
Mia used him as a chair.
Max laughed so hard he forgot about the timer.
Command four: tiny.
Max stepped on him.
Leo respawned. Full size. Waving.
Command five: fling.
Leo flew across the entire map...
and landed on the winners' podium.
He took a bow.
Ten seconds left.
Max saved the best for last.
The one command nobody comes back from.
Ban.
He typed it slowly. Carefully.
B. A. N. Space. L. E. O.
He pressed enter.
"You are not an admin."
Round over. One second too late.
Leo was still here.
And next round...
Leo got admin.
