At RDC, Roblox's yearly developer conference, Roblox announced a web player.
By the end of this year, you'll be able to join games in your browser on Chrome.
No app. No install.
More browsers are coming later.
And it all starts with a link.
Search, YouTube, X, even an email from a friend.
Click it, and you start playing instantly.
That's not all.
Offline play is coming too.
Game creators will be able to add solo modes that work with no internet.
On a plane, on the train, anywhere.
That's planned for mid-2027, with early access coming soon.
