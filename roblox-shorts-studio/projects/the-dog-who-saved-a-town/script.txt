In 1925, a town in Alaska is running out of time.
Kids are getting sick, and the medicine is nearly seven hundred miles away.
Ships can't get through the ice. Planes can't fly in that cold.
So they send dogs.

Twenty sled teams will pass it along, like a relay.
One team gets the hardest part.
Its leader is a twelve-year-old dog called Togo.

They run a hundred and seventy miles just to reach it...
then turn around and race back.
Togo takes a shortcut across a frozen sea, in a blizzard.
That night, the storm blows all that ice out to sea.
They crossed it just in time.

Two hundred and sixty-one miles... more than any other team.
Five and a half days later, the medicine arrives, and the outbreak is stopped.

But the dog who ran the last fifty-three miles gets the statue in New York.
His name was Balto.
Togo had to wait seventy-six years for his.
