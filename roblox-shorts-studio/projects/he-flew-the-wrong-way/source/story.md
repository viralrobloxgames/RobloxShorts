# He Flew The Wrong Way (standalone story, one part)

Mode: true story told as grounded action (references/story.md). Hook: takes off for California, lands in Ireland. One
clear rule: the officials said no, so he files a plan that the officials can't refuse and flies the other way; every
later beat is the officials catching up with a man who simply says "my compass". Attempts and obstacles: no radio, an
old compass, fuel leaking into the cockpit (screwdriver through the floor). Payoff: the "punishment" ends the day his
ship gets home, New York throws a bigger parade than Lindbergh's, and he never admits a thing (reframes the hook's
"heading for California"). Third person. Picked for the user's overnight run of 2026-10-05 (named by the user; facts
verified below). Script: `../script.txt` (156 words with the CTA, one sentence per line).

Cast: Max plays Douglas Corrigan (bareheaded, big grin, a leather jacket colour where possible); Mia the US aviation
official (officer_cap) who refuses permission and sends the telegram; Leo the Irish officer at Baldonnel
(officer_cap); Skye and Noob ground crew / parade crowd. Route: web (three.js).

## The facts (kept accurate; sources below)
- Douglas Corrigan bought a 1929 Curtiss Robin monoplane in 1933 for $310 and rebuilt it (about $900 in all), named
  *Sunshine* (Wikipedia). Script: "too old and too patched up".
- He applied to fly the Atlantic nonstop; in 1935 the Bureau of Air Commerce refused: "his plane was deemed unsound
  for a nonstop transatlantic trip" (Wikipedia); denied again in later years (NPS: "denied by aviation authorities";
  taraross / historynet: refused "every time"). Script: "For years, he'd asked to fly the Atlantic. Officials said no."
- July 1938: he flew Long Beach to New York nonstop (27 h), then filed a flight plan for a nonstop return to Long Beach,
  California (NPS, Wikipedia).
- 17 July 1938, 05:15, Floyd Bennett Field, Brooklyn: took off east with 320 US gallons of fuel and kept going east
  into cloud (Wikipedia; NPS "at dawn"). Script: "At dawn, he took off, and kept flying east. Into the clouds."
- "He had no radio and his compass was 20 years old." (Wikipedia). Script: "No radio. A twenty-year-old compass."
- "A gasoline leak developed, filling the cockpit with fumes"; he "used a screwdriver to punch a hole through the
  cockpit floor so that the fuel would drain away" (from the hot exhaust) (Wikipedia).
- 18 July 1938: landed at Baldonnel Aerodrome, County Dublin, after 28 h 13 min (Wikipedia). On landing: "Just got
  in from New York. Where am I?" and "I left New York yesterday morning headed for California, but I got mixed up in
  the clouds" (NPS; History.com). He claimed he misread his compass in the cloud and only noticed after about 26 h
  (Wikipedia).
- "Aviation officials sent a 600-word telegram to list the regulations broken by his flight" (Wikipedia). Script: "a
  six-hundred-word telegram of rules he broke".
- Punishment: pilot's certificate suspended for 14 days, ending 4 August, the day he arrived back in New York on the
  liner *Manhattan* with his crated plane (Wikipedia; NPS "suspended until August 4"). Script: "No flying... until his
  ship got home."
- New York gave him a ticker-tape parade up Broadway; "more people attended his Broadway ticker-tape parade than had
  honored Lindbergh" (Wikipedia; taraross: over a million). He also met President Roosevelt.
- "He never publicly admitted to having flown to Ireland intentionally" (Wikipedia). Script ends there; nothing about
  his later life beyond that.
