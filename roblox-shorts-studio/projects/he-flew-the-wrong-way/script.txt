A pilot takes off from New York, heading for California.
Twenty-eight hours later, he lands in Ireland.

For years, he'd asked to fly the Atlantic.
Officials said no. His plane was too old and too patched up.

So he filed a flight plan to California.
At dawn, he took off, and kept flying east. Into the clouds.

No radio. A twenty-year-old compass.
Then fuel leaked into the cockpit.
He punched a hole in the floor with a screwdriver to drain it.

He lands in Dublin, climbs out, and says:
"Just got in from New York. Where am I?"

His excuse? In the clouds, he misread his compass.

Officials sent a six-hundred-word telegram of rules he broke.
His punishment? No flying... until his ship got home.

New York threw him a ticker-tape parade. Bigger than Lindbergh's.

They called him Wrong Way Corrigan.
And he never admitted a thing.
