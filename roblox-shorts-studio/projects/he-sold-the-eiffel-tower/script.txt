This man sold the Eiffel Tower.
And the buyer never told the police.

Paris, 1925. Newspapers say the tower is rusting, and fixing it costs a fortune.
Victor Lustig reads that and gets an idea.

He fakes government letters and invites scrap metal dealers to a fancy hotel.
"The tower is coming down," he whispers. "It's a secret. Who wants the metal?"

One dealer, André Poisson, wants it badly.
So Lustig asks him for a little bribe too.

Poisson pays. Lustig grabs the cash and takes a train out of the country.
And Poisson? He's too embarrassed to tell anyone.

So Lustig comes back and tries it again!
This time a dealer calls the police.
He flees to America, and even tricks the gangster Al Capone.

Then he's caught making fake money, escapes down a rope, and gets caught again.

His death certificate says: apprentice salesman and counterfeiter.
