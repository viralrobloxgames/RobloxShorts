One of them is a copy.
And it wasn't late anymore.

Then Mia joined the server.
She'd been spectating the whole time.
And she'd noticed something.
It doesn't copy you. It mirrors you.
Left becomes right. Right becomes left.

So she gave one order.
Raise your right hand.
Left Max raised his right hand.
Right Max raised his left.
Just like the star on its hoodie.
If you said left... you were right.

The copy looked at Mia.
And it smiled.
Then it sprinted for the door.
Max went after it. Too slow.
Leo was standing right in the doorway.
Close the door, Leo!
Leo didn't close the door.
He waved.
And the copy waved back. Half a second late.

The player list updated.
Four players online.
Max. Mia. Leo.
And Leo.
