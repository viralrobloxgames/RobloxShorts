Two Maxes. Same face. Same name.
One of them is a copy.
And it wasn't late anymore.

But Mia had been spectating the whole time.
It doesn't copy you, she said. It mirrors you.

So she gave one order.
Raise your right hand.
Left Max raised his right hand.
Right Max raised his left.
Just like the star on its hoodie.
If you said left... you were right.

The copy stared at Mia.
Then it came for her.
Max jumped between them.
Mia whispered. Walk backwards.
Max stepped back. It stepped back, toward the door.
Another step. And another.
One step from the door... it stopped.

It was fighting the mirror.
So Max sprinted backwards. Full speed.
And a mirror has to follow.
Straight through the door.
Mia slammed it shut.

The player list updated.
Two players online. Max. Mia.
Max waved goodbye at the door.
Nothing waved back.
