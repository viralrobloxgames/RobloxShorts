# The Backwards Umbrella (standalone story, one part)

Mode: power with a downside, third person (references/story.md). Script: `../script.txt` (152 words with the CTA;
expect about 61-62 s of speech, so a 62-64 s video: inside the 61-65 s rule). Setting: a sunny park (new set, not the
classroom). Cast: Leo (the umbrella), Max, Mia, the Noob, a dog, a crowd at the barbecue. Route: web (three.js).

## The rule (one, kept consistent)
When Leo's umbrella is open, it rains everywhere except under it. Closed: no rain, instant sunshine.
The downside: it soaks everyone around him, and it only shelters as many as fit underneath.
The payoff uses the same rule: rain everywhere is exactly what a fire needs. Ending reframes the opening: the one
place that stays dry is the one place Mia wants to be.

## Beats
1. Hook (frame 1): blue sky, Leo pops the umbrella open and a dark cloud slams in over the whole park; rain pours on
   everyone, Leo stands in a perfect dry circle. Reads on mute in a second.
2. Max, Mia (hair flattened), a passing dog (shakes itself): soaked. Leo closes it: cloud gone, sun back.
3. Sharing: Max and Mia squeeze under (three fit, shoulders touching, side-on). The Noob, outside, gets a tiny personal
   thundercloud with a lightning flash; hair sticking up.
4. Leo keeps it closed (umbrella under his arm, the others eyeing it).
5. Park barbecue: bunting, table, bouncy castle. Max flips one burger: the grill erupts (flame column, "FWOOMP").
   Flames jump to the table, napkins, the bouncy castle (it sags). Crowd screams and runs (real runs, distance-driven).
6. Leo opens the umbrella: rain hammers everything; grill hisses and steams; fire out. Thirty soaked people cheer
   (proud / laugh poses, no two-arms-up) for the one dry boy.
7. Mia ducks under: "Room for one more?" (speech bubble). Leo shuffles over. Wide: rain on everyone else, two dry
   kids under one umbrella. Hold the beat.
8. CTA: "Follow Viral Roblox Games for more stories like this." + end card (@viralrobloxgames, FOLLOW).
