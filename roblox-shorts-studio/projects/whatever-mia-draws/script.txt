Whatever Mia draws with her pencil comes to life.
Exactly the way she draws it.
And Mia can't draw.

Her dog is four sticks and a circle. It still fetches.
Her bike has square wheels. Bonk. Bonk.
She drew herself a boyfriend. He's a stick. He's very supportive.

Leo, the best artist in class, laughs.
"Give me that. I'll show you how it's done."

He draws a lion.
Every hair. Every tooth.
It's perfect.
It roars.

The class screams.
The lion corners Leo.
Mia snatches the pencil back.

She draws a cage around it.
The bars are wobbly, but they hold.
She forgot to draw a roof.
The lion jumps straight out.

Her stick boyfriend steps in front of Leo.
The lion bats him across the floor like a toy.

Mia stops.
It's not a monster.
It's a big cat.
And there's one thing anyone can draw.

She scribbles.
A ball of yarn rolls across the floor.
The lion pounces on it, rolls over and purrs.

For the first time, Mia drew something perfectly.

Leo reaches for the pencil.
Mia pulls it away.
"No. You're too good."
