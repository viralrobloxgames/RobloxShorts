Anything drawn with Mia's pencil comes to life.
Exactly as it's drawn.
And Mia can't draw.

Her dog is four sticks. It still fetches.
Her bike has square wheels. Bonk. Bonk.
Her boyfriend is a stick. Very supportive.

Then Leo, the class artist, grabs the pencil.
"Watch and learn."

He draws a lion.
Every hair. Every tooth.
It's perfect.
It roars.

The class screams. The lion corners Leo.
Mia grabs the pencil and draws a cage.
The bars are wobbly, but they hold.
She forgot the roof.
The lion jumps straight out.

Her stick boyfriend steps in.
The lion bats him around like a toy.
Mia stops. It's just a big cat.
And there's one thing anyone can draw.

She scribbles.
A ball of yarn.
The lion pounces and purrs.

For once, Mia drew something perfectly.

Leo wants another go.
"No. You're too good."
