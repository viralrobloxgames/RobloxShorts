Leo had admin commands for sixty seconds... and he's already falling.
Rewind.
Leo won a giveaway in Mega Obby: admin powers, one minute only.
Command one: speed. Leo shot forward so fast he flew past the first jump. And off the map.
Respawn. Command two: giant. Now every jump was one step. Max and Mia watched him stroll past stage ten.
Then came a tiny tunnel. Giant Leo did not fit. Giant Leo was stuck.
Command three: shrink. He slipped right through... and one gust of wind blew him all the way back to stage one.
Twenty seconds left. Mia typed in chat, "just use fly."
So Leo typed fly. He rose past every stage, past the clouds, right up to the winner's pad.
Down below, Max and Mia actually started clapping.
Leo reached for the edge.
Three... two... one.
Admin expired. One block from the finish.
Leo looked down, waved goodbye... and fell.
That's how Leo lost Mega Obby. With admin.
