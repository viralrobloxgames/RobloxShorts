# Boundary sheet

The contract between the eleven chapters, which are authored in parallel by different sub-agents. A chapter's **first
frame** must match its "Start" column and its **last frame** must match its "End" column. Anything between is the
chapter's own business, but it may only use the cast, wardrobe, sets, lighting and props of the shared kit, by the ids
below. The stage-6 reviewer checks every seam against this sheet.

Every break is a hard cut (no fades, no music; music is laid once over the stitched video). Chapters 2-11 open on a
full-width card (DAY and clock time) over the first shot for about 2 s, with Skye's VO naming the day from frame 0.
Chapter 1 opens on the hook with only a small time stamp. Each chapter ends on its last line plus 0.5-1.0 s of room tone.
