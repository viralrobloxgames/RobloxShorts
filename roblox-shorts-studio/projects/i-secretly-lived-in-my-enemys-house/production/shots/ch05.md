# Ch05 shot plan: The Tea Party (WEDNESDAY 3:41 PM)

Set: attic only (`K.sets.attic`, `setState({ chapter: 5 })`), light `attic_afternoon` throughout (low warm sun through
the round window, dust shafts). Cast: Skye (`skye_hoodie` + `backpack` until S9, then backpack off, lying in the nest),
Lily (`lily_day`, `teddy` in every shot). Max and Dad offscreen. Est. length 72-80 s; every time below is an estimate
and is retimed from `audio/chapters/ch05/lines.json` when narration lands.

## Geography and the 180-degree line
Two axes, one per half of the chapter:
- **Nest <-> hatch** (S1-S11): Skye in the nest under the round window, Lily at the hatch, later walking in. Cameras stay
  on the window-wall-left side of the line (camera never crosses between them): Skye always frames screen-left,
  Lily screen-right.
- **Tea box** (S12-S30): upturned box a few studs in front of the nest. Skye cross-legged on the nest side
  (screen-left), Lily kneeling on the hatch side (screen-right), the round window behind Skye for back light. Same side
  of the line as the first half, so the cut into the tea party doesn't flip anyone.

Faces always 3/4 to camera; over-shoulders keep the near person's cheek/ear, never a full back of head filling frame.
