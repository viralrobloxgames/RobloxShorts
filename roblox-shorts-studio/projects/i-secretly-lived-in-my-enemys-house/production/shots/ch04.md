# Ch4 shot plan: "Cinnamon" (WEDNESDAY 12:15 PM)

Set: `classroom` only, light `school_day` throughout. Owner: ch04. Times are estimates (VO 2.9 w/s, Max 2.7, gaps
0.3 s); retimed to `lines.json` / `captions.json` when the narration lands. Estimated total ~62-66 s at 30 fps.

## Geography (one side of the line, all chapter)

Skye sits at `desk_skye` (row 2, window side), facing the board. Max's desk `desk_max` is row 3, one row behind and one
column toward the aisle (diagonally behind her). Max stands at `desk_skye_aisle`: in the aisle beside Skye's desk, facing
her (heading toward the window). The 180° line runs Skye ↔ Max; **every camera sits on the board side of it** (in front
of the desks, looking back/across), so Skye is always screen-left looking right and Max screen-right looking left, and
both faces read 3/4. No camera is ever behind Skye's head or Max's head.

Extras (3-4, silent): row 1 and row 2/3 aisle-side desks, eating (sandwich / apple / juice box on desks, a slow
bite-chew loop, low amplitude; held between lines so frames repeat).

## Wardrobe / persistent things

- Skye: `skye_hoodie`, `cobweb` on the LEFT of her hair all chapter (end frame too), sitting. `backpack` hanging on the
  back of her chair (not worn). Nothing on her desk until she takes half the sandwich.
- Max: `max_school`. Lunch: `cookie` in his RIGHT hand, a crustless sandwich (two halves, `sandwich_half` x2, or a
  `sandwich_crustless` that splits) in his LEFT on a napkin. Leaves the cookie on Skye's desk when she won't take it
  (S8) so his right hand is free for the half-sandwich hand-off.
- Extras: `extras` looks, lunch props on their desks.
