# Ch2 "Twelve Pancakes" (TUESDAY 6:04 AM): shot plan

Owner: `ch02`. Clip: `web/ch02.js`. Times are estimates (VO/SKYE 2.9 w/s, MAX 2.7, LILY 2.6, DAD 2.5, 0.25 s gaps,
0.35 s on a scene change); every cut is re-anchored to `audio/chapters/ch02/lines.json` when narration lands.
Estimated length ~70 s. Three scenes (+ one exterior insert): hallway (predawn) -> kitchen (predawn, warm kitchen light)
-> back step outside (predawn) -> classroom (school_day).

Rules kept throughout: camera on whoever speaks, faces 3/4 to camera, no backs of heads; 180-degree line in the kitchen
is the island's long axis seen from the stairs side (Dad/stove camera-left, Skye's hiding side always toward camera
in the "hide" shots, never crossing); `speak()` faces on every word; real walks (`travelTo`) only; one-arm reaches/waves
only, never two arms up; held props at the palm via `K.hold()`.

## Wardrobe / props for the whole chapter
- Skye `skye_hoodie` + `backpack` (on both shoulders all chapter, also in class).
- Dad `dad_apron`, `spatula` in his right hand all kitchen scene (frying pan on the stove).
- Max `max_pjs` in the kitchen, `max_school` in class. Hair flat on one side if the kit offers a bedhead state (optional).
- Lily `lily_pjs`, `teddy` in hand (as `loadCast()` gives it), always.
- Pancakes: stack of 12 on the island (kitchen `setState({ chapter: 2, pancakes: 12 })`), 11 from the steal; the stolen
  `pancake` in Skye's right hand, then in her mouth (crawl), then in her hand outside.
- Fridge: scattered magnet letters with LILY spelled out (set state ch2).
