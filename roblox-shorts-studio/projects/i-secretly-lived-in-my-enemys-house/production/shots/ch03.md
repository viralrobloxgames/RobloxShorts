# Ch3 shot plan: "A Useful Ghost" (TUESDAY 11:52 PM)

Owner: `ch03`. Set: kitchen only (`kit-sets-c`, world offset (900,0,0)). Light: `night_fridge` (fridge open = key
light; fridge shut = moonlight through the back-door window only) plus Max's `flashlight` beam while he holds it.
Times below are estimates (~2.4 words/s); retimed from `audio/chapters/ch03/lines.json` when narration lands.
Screen direction (180-degree line): the line runs fridge <-> island. Cameras stay on the island/back-door side of it
(fridge on frame left, pantry frame right of the fridge, stairs at the far right). Skye's eyeline to the fridge = left.

Pack animations used: `idle`, `walk`, `run`, `duck` (crouch behind the island), `horror_reach` (letter onto the
fridge / reaching to the fridge door), `horror_listen` (freeze at the footsteps), `horror_torch_hold` (Max's
flashlight arm), `think`, `typing` (sandwich hands at the counter), `talk`, `shrug`, `scheming`, `look_up`, `hold`.
No two-arms-up poses. Walks: `travel`/`travelTo` (walk 12, Dad shuffle = walk at 8 studs/s with distance-driven legs).
