# Ch7 "The Pumpkin Girl": shot plan (FRIDAY 4:05 PM)

Owner: `ch07`. Clip: `web/ch07.js`. Light: `attic_afternoon` throughout. Set: attic only, `setState({ chapter: 7 })`
but with the pumpkin bucket and the vacuum driven by the chapter (see Kit needs). Times are estimates (about 72 s,
24 lines); they are retimed from `audio/chapters/ch07/lines.json` when the narration lands.

## Geography and the 180-degree line

The attic is one room, staged as three spots on one side of an axis line that runs from the **tea party** (upturned box,
by the nest under the round window) to the **hatch** in the floor:

- **Tea party**: Skye (`tea_skye`, cross-legged) and Lily (`tea_lily`, kneeling/sitting opposite), tea set on the box.
- **Decor line**: HALLOWEEN box (open), `skeleton` and `witch` standing beside it with a clear one-character gap
  between them: mark `decor_gap` (Skye's "pumpkin girl" spot), heading toward the hatch/camera side.
- **Hatch**: lid hinged on one side; climbers rise through it (`hatch_below` -> `hatch_top`); the vacuum ends at
  `vacuum_by_hatch`.

All cameras stay on the round-window side of the line (looking from the window/nest side across toward the hatch and the
decorations), so Dad is always screen right of Skye when they face each other and Lily is screen left of Skye. Reverse
angles are cheated 3/4, never across the line.

## Wardrobe, props, faces (whole chapter)

- Skye: `skye_hoodie`, no backpack (it's in the nest). From 0:07 the `pumpkin_bucket` on her head (crooked as Lily jams it,
  tilted ~12 deg; Max straightens it to level at ~0:58). Face held still while she plays decoration: `neutral` frozen,
  eyes wide, with a tiny flick to `scared` when the nozzle comes up.
- Lily: `lily_day`, `teddy` in her left hand in every shot.
- Dad: `dad_cardigan` (no apron), `vacuum`.
- Max: `max_school`.
- Tea set (`teapot`, two `cups`) on the upturned box all chapter; Skye's cup in her hand at frame 0, set down when she
  stands.
