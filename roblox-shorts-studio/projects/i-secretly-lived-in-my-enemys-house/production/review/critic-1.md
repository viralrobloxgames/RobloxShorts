# critic-1: frame-by-frame review, ch01 + ch02 (film 0:00-2:10)

How I looked: I joined chNN_a + chNN_b, took 3 fps 2x2 sheets at 960 px across every shot, then went to every frame (1 fps step) or full-res crops wherever there was movement, contact or a cut. Frame numbers `fN` are 1-based chapter render frames (ch01 f1 = film 0:00.000; ch02 f1 = film 1:03.700). Times are film times.

## ch01 "The Dare" (0:00-1:03.7)

### A. Staging: the hook "hiding" doesn't work (shared cause: Skye is in the open gap of an open closet and Max is right next to her)
1. **ch01 0:00.0-0:02.5 (f1-75), staging.** The closet leaf is open about 40%. Skye stands in the moonlit gap, and Max walks straight at her with his eyes on her (f5-25). In the CU (f35-75) Max sits at frame left, about 2 m away, looking straight at her. Nothing hides her. **Fix:** close the leaf to a crack and put Skye behind the closed leaf, between the hoodies and in shadow. Have Max look at the door or the floor (sweeping the flashlight), not at her face. If he must stand close, turn him 3/4 away. **must**
2. **ch01 0:03.5-0:07.1 (f105-215), staging + caption logic.** Max's head is about 0.5 m behind Skye's shoulder, facing her. At f145 he smiles toward her while saying "Is somebody in my closet?". "He had no idea" (f105-135) plays over him staring at her from arm's length. **Fix:** same restage as #1. Keep Max at least 2 m away, outside the door, and his eyes off her until the door opens. **must**
3. **ch01 0:10.1-0:10.6 (f305-318), interpenetration + staging.** After he shuts the door, Skye's purple backpack, a white arm block and her pink hair stick out *through* the closed louvred door. In a crop of f315 the backpack sits in front of the door leaf, next to the knobs, in full view as Max turns away. **Fix:** move Skye 30-40 cm deeper into the closet so nothing crosses the door plane, or cheat the door forward. **must**
4. **ch01 0:07.8-0:08.4 (f235-250), props/readability.** The "lock of pink hair" insert renders as a detached, fleshy pink tentacle standing up out of a grey box against yellow/red/white panels. It reads as a creature, not hair between hoodies, and no hoodies are visible. **Fix:** frame two hoodies with the hair lock between them, flatter lighting, and the lock hanging down. **should**
5. **ch01 0:01.1-0:11.5 (f35-345), poses.** Skye's hand over her mouth is a giant block slab covering about 40% of the frame in the CU (f35-135, f325-345). At f345->f355 it vanishes in one frame with no lowering motion (pop). Max's flashlight arm is held dead straight out like a zombie (f5-25, f215-225). **Fix:** pull the camera back or lower the hand so it sits at mouth height in the lower third. Animate the hand down over 6-8 frames. Bend Max's flashlight elbow. **should**

### B. Classroom: furniture interpenetration (shared cause: Max's marks are inside the desk/chair volumes, and his walk is a straight line through the furniture)
6. **ch01 0:12.5-0:16.0 (f375-480), interpenetration / standing inside the chair (user's 0:15).** Max stands upright in his row, and the **blue chair back runs through his chest from collar to hips** for the whole shot (f385-480). His hips are below the desk top. The adjacent desk top enters his right forearm. In the wide (f375) his feet are inside the chair. He is also a static mannequin for 3 s (arms dead straight, no weight shift). **Fix:** either seat him properly (hips on the seat, chair back *behind* him, knees under the desk), or stand him in the aisle beside the chair. Add idle breathing/weight shift. **must**
7. **ch01 0:15.8-0:16.9 (f476-508), walking through furniture.** As he leaves his desk his legs pass through the blue chair (f482-485) and his hip clips the front desk corner (f500-506). Arms swing out wide and robotic. **Fix:** step out sideways into the aisle, then walk. **must**
8. **ch01 0:16.9-0:17.0 (f509-511), pop.** The new CU opens on the *back* of Max's head (f509-510), then the same setup has him facing camera at f511: a 180° flip in 1-2 frames. **Fix:** trim f509-510 or start the CU on the facing pose. **must**
9. **ch01 0:45.1-0:46.9 (f1356-1410), walking through desks (user's 0:45).** Max stands up and walks diagonally *through* the desks and chairs. At f1372 he's inside a chair. At f1392 the desk top cuts his waist with his legs below it. At f1396 a desk passes through his torso. At f1400 he walks through the front desk. **Fix:** route him to the aisle and down it (the aisle between rows 2 and 3 is clear), at least one desk-width from any desk. **must**

### C. Classroom: props and contact
10. **ch01 0:24.5-0:24.9 (f737-749), props/logic.** In the top-down insert the spider crawls out from under the lunchbox edge and appears on the sandwich (f746-749). No hand drops it, and Max's hand isn't in shot. Max's teal torso also sits *over/into* the desk edge at left (f737-749). **Fix:** show Max's hand (palm down, spider in fingers) entering and releasing above the sandwich. Pull his body back from the desk edge. **must**
11. **ch01 0:25.3-0:27.3 (f750-820), pose + logic (user's 0:25).** On "Spider! Get it off!" Skye's arms fling out to both sides (two-arms-up/T), breaking the house rule. Her right hand goes into or behind the lunchbox. The spider sits on the *sandwich on the desk*, so "get it off" makes no sense. **Fix:** recoil pose: lean back, *one* hand up at her chest, the other gripping the desk edge. Either put the spider on her sleeve/shoulder or change the action to her shoving the lunchbox away. **must**
12. **ch01 0:27.5-0:30.1 (f825-900), hand/prop through body (user's 0:34 class).** Max holds the spider up "by her face", but his **fist is pressed into Skye's cheek/face**: in full-res f845 the fist block overlaps her head and the spider dangles at her mouth. His forearm crosses over the desk. **Fix:** hold the spider 15-20 cm in front of her face, above the desk, fist clear of her head, elbow bent. **must**
13. **ch01 0:17.2-0:44.8 (f515-1345, every shot of the pair), floating props.** Skye's purple lunchbox + sandwich float at *shoulder height* beside her, about 30 cm above her desk top (clearest at full-res f960: the desk top is at waist level and the lunchbox hangs in the air beside Max's chin). Visible at f515-640, f905-1105, f1315-1345. **Fix:** put the lunchbox on the desk top (snap it to desk height), and keep the CUs consistent with that. **must**
14. **ch01 0:31.4-0:34.5 (f945-1035), interpenetration.** In the standing 2-shot, the desk between them runs into both bodies: the corner of the desk top enters Skye's right arm and Max's left side (f960). **Fix:** move them apart by one desk-width, or move the desk. **must**
15. **ch01 0:17.2-0:21.6 and 0:37-0:43.4 (f515-645, f1115-1305), camera.** A giant out-of-focus white block (Skye's forearm/shoulder) fills the bottom-left third of Max's CUs, and a white blob sits at the left edge (f1285-1305). **Fix:** move the OTS camera over her shoulder, not through her arm, or crop the arm out. **should**
16. **ch01 0:27.3-0:28.0 (f820-830), emotion pop.** Skye goes from full scream (f815) to deadpan in one cut, while the spider is still in her face. **Fix:** hold a few frames of panic easing into annoyance. **should**

### D. Back door
17. **ch01 0:50.8-0:51.3 (f1524-1541), walking through a door.** Skye reaches toward the back door, and at f1530-1539 her body merges into the closed door leaf and frame. The door never swings open, and she ghosts through it. **Fix:** animate the door opening inward (hinge side visible), her stepping through the gap, and the door closing behind her. **must**
18. **ch01 0:50.1 (f1505), camera.** Her near arm swings huge across the lower-left of the frame. **should**

### E. Night: identity and caption logic
19. **ch01 0:55.1-1:01.4 (f1655-1840), identity (user's 0:57).** Under the blue night light Max in bed has a dark brown face and blue-black hair, and reads as a different boy. Same in the hook (f255-295 "Huh. Just hoodies", f115-205). Skye's face also goes dark brown in every closet shot (f1-365, f1575-1645). **Fix:** add a warm key/fill on the faces (bedside lamp or a moon rim plus face fill) so skin reads peach and Max's hair reads brown. Keep the blue on the room, not the skin. **must**
20. **ch01 0:57.3-0:59.4 (f1720-1785), caption/speaker.** Lily's line "I am in bed! And you're too loud!" (yellow) plays over a CU of *Max*. Lily is never shown, and the cut has no motivation. **Fix:** cut to Lily sitting up in her bed (or to Max turning toward the wall with her voice muffled through it, captioned as off-screen). **must**
21. **ch01 0:55.1-0:57.1 (f1655-1715), sitting in bed.** Max's torso rises straight out of a flat blanket: no leg volume, and the torso is cut off at the blanket plane. **Fix:** add a leg bump under the blanket and lean the torso back against the headboard. **should**
22. **ch01 1:01.4-1:03.7 (f1845-1911), last frame.** Skye stands fully lit in the door gap, with an oversized white arm block on the left leaf (f1845). The blue book on the floor hovers (its shadow is detached). Fine as a "scheming" end beat, but drop the book onto the floor and shrink the arm. **should**
