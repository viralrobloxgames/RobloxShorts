## ch03 "A Useful Ghost" (film 2:10-3:17)

### Staging / secrets
- **ch03 0:12.6-0:42.2 [2:22.6-2:52.2] (f379-1267), staging, MUST.** "Hidden" Skye is in plain view through the
  pantry louvres in nearly every Max shot. The louvre gaps are about half open, and she stands upright right behind the
  door, moonlit, so her pink hair, face, white hoodie and jeans are all readable: 12.6-13.6 and 15.3-21.6 (Max
  at the fridge, pantry 1-2 studs behind his shoulder), 25.0-30.0, the fridge-open shot 30.0-30.5, and the whole
  sandwich scene 32.5-42.2. In that scene Max faces screen-left toward the pantry and she is face-on to him
  (full-res f1007). Fix: make the pantry door read as solid (overlapping slats with no see-through, or a solid
  panel) and keep the pantry interior dark. Put Skye deep inside the pantry and only show her in the `pantry_gap`
  CU. Re-angle `island_counter` so the pantry is behind the camera or out of frame.
- **ch03 0:49.5-0:51.5 [2:59.5-3:01.5] (f1487-1547), staging, MUST.** Skye "ducks" at the stairs end of the island,
  on the open camera side, crouched in the middle of the floor. Dad is coming down the stairs facing straight at her
  (full-res f1525). Nothing is between them. Fix: duck on the far side of the island (the side away from the stairs
  and fridge path) so the island blocks Dad's line of sight, and keep her there for shots 22-25.

### Interpenetration / walking

    through the balusters and handrail.
  - ch03 0:50.2-0:51.5 [3:00.2-3:01.5] (f1507-1547): Dad walks down **in mid-air** beside the stairs, outside the
    handrail, with the balusters through his torso (full-res f1525).
  - Fix: move the `stairs_top`→`stairs_bottom` path onto the tread centre-line (feet on the treads) and re-check every
    stair use in the film. ch04 and later chapters use the same kitchen stairs.
- **ch03 0:09.6-0:10.0 [2:19.6-2:20.0] (f290-301), interpenetration, MUST.** Skye runs into the pantry *through* the
  louvred door panel: her torso is behind the slats while her arm and leg are in front. Fix: swing the door open
  first, then path her through the opening and close it to ajar.
- **ch03 0:16.8-0:18.6 [2:26.8-2:28.6] (f505-558), interpenetration, MUST.** While reading the letters, Max's right
  forearm sinks into the fridge door (full-res f526: the arm is cut by the door plane and the letters show beside
  it). Fix: put him 0.5 stud back, or use a lean pose with the hand flat on the door surface.
- **ch03 0:44.4-0:49.2 [2:54.4-2:59.2] (f1333-1477), props + interpenetration, MUST.**
