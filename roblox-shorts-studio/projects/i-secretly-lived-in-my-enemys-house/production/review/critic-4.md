# critic-4: frame-by-frame critic, ch07 + ch08 (film 6:23.8-8:35)

Method: ch07_a+b and ch08_a+b joined per chapter; 3 fps 2x2 sheets at 960 px over every second of both chapters,
plus 5-10 fps strips over every movement / contact beat (hatch climbs, vacuum, Max's arrival, ch08 door and attic).
Times are **chapter time** (`chNN mm:ss.s`), frames are **chapter frames** (1-based, 30 fps, segment B continues the
count, e.g. ch07 SPLIT 993), and the film time is in brackets. Severity: **must** = a viewer will notice;
**should** = weakens the scene.

## ch07 "The Pumpkin Girl" (film 6:23.8-7:37.3, 2204 frames)

The chapter's core gag (Skye frozen as a decoration while Dad inspects her) is staged so that the bodies of every
character who comes near her pass through her. The decor line (skeleton | gap | witch) is too narrow for a
scarecrow pose: Skye's outstretched arms go into the skeleton on one side and into whoever stands on the other side
(Lily, then Dad, then Max). Fix that once (widen the gap: move skeleton and witch ~1.5 studs further out each, or
shorten the pose, see C7-2) and half the issues below go away.

### Shared causes
- **C7-1 Decor gap too narrow for the pose (every decor shot 0:11.7-1:13.4, f351-2204).** Skye's right arm passes
  through the skeleton's shoulder/ribcage in every decor shot (f361-490, f901-1051, f1060-1720, f2011-2191); her left
  arm passes into whoever stands at her left (Lily f370-471, Dad f950-1720, Max f1861-1940). Fix: widen `decor_gap`
  (skeleton and witch each ~1.5 studs out) and put Dad/Max/Lily marks **in front of** her (facing her, ~2 studs off),
  never shoulder to shoulder. **must**
- **C7-2 The "scarecrow" pose is a literal T-pose held for ~60 s (f361-2204).** The brief bans T-poses; this one
  is arms dead straight at shoulder height, palms down, rigid, for the whole back half of the chapter. Fix: keep the
  idea but break the T: upper arms ~20 deg below horizontal, elbows bent ~25 deg, wrists drooped (a "propped
  scarecrow"), plus one small idle sway; it still reads as a decoration and stops hitting neighbours. **must**
- **C7-3 Face pops / wrong expression.** Several speakers cycle smile -> "o" -> scowl every 0.3 s through one line
  (Dad f530-581, f601-631, f1281-1351, f1441-1471; Max f1861-1930), and Skye wears a cheerful red-lipstick smile while
  panicking/being hoovered/complaining (f131-291, f1450-1551, f1981-1990). Fix: one base emotion per line (per the shot
  plan), with lip-sync only on the mouth; Skye `scared` f131-291, a `scared` flick for the nozzle, `annoyed` for "I
  almost got hoovered". **must**
